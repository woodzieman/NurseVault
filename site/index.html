What You Can Do

📁

Documents & Folders

Import PDFs, images, text/RTF files up to 48 MB. Drag-and-drop on Mac/iPad. Folders import as nested vault folders.

💊

Drug Reference

Built-in Merck Manual dataset — 2,383 drugs, ~350 MB. Search by name or content. Always offline, never synced.

📝

Notes & Scanning

Create text notes, scan printed references with on-device OCR, and take photos — all saved locally.

🔍

Full-Text Search

Find documents by title, filename, notes, or file content (PDFs, text). Even on Apple Watch.

☁️

Private iCloud Sync

Sync via your own CloudKit private database. No third-party servers. Only you can access your data.

🫀

Code Blue Flows

ACL protocols, resuscitation checklists, and critical-care flows — always at your fingertips.

How It Works

1

Install Nurse Vault on your Apple devices. No account, no sign-up, no subscription.

2

Import your clinical reference materials — PDFs, lab sheets, drug labels, photos — via drag-and-drop or the import menu.

3

Your data syncs privately across your own devices via iCloud (CloudKit). Everything works 100 % offline.
