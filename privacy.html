Privacy Policy

Nurse Vault — Woodzieman

Last updated: October 7, 2026

1. Overview

Nurse Vault ("the app," "we," "our") is designed to respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights. By using Nurse Vault, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Data We Collect

Nurse Vault does not collect any personal data.

  • No accounts or user identifiers are created.
  • No analytics, crash reports, or advertising SDKs are included.
  • No data is transmitted to our servers (we operate no servers).

3. Data You Create

Any content you add to the app (documents, notes, photos, scanned references) is stored exclusally on your device and, if you opt in, synced to your own private Apple iCloud (CloudKit private database). This data is:

  • Accessible only by your Apple ID.
  • Never accessible by us or any third party.
  • Never transmitted outside Apple's infrastructure.

4. Camera Access

Nurse Vault requests camera access solely to allow you to capture photos of reference materials and to use on-device OCR to read and save printed text. The camera stream is not recorded, transmitted, or stored by the app outside your device. You grant this permission via standard iOS prompts and can revoke it at any time in Settings → Privacy → Camera.

5. The Bundled Drug Reference

The app includes a bundled drug-reference dataset sourced from the Merck Manual Professional. This dataset ships within the app bundle, operates entirely offline, and is never synced or transmitted. It is provided for informational purposes and is not a substitute for professional medical advice.

6. Third-Party Services

Nurse Vault does not integrate with any third-party services, analytics platforms, advertising networks, or SDKs. The only cloud service used is iCloud (CloudKit), governed by Apple's Privacy Policy, solely for syncing your personal data across devices you own.

7. Children's Privacy

Nurse Vault is intended for healthcare professionals. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. The app does not require an account or age verification.

8. Data Security

Because we collect no data, there is no risk of a data breach involving personal information. Your on-device data is protected by iOS device encryption. iCloud sync uses Apple's end-to-end encryption for the CloudKit private database.

9. Your Rights

  • Access / Delete: Remove the app from your device to delete all stored data. In iCloud, delete via Settings → [Your Name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.
  • Revoke Permissions: Turn off camera access at Settings → Privacy → Camera.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The most current version will always be available at this page. Your continued use of Nurse Vault after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
