Notes
  • For most accurate calculation, input fractional ages where possible, especially for young individuals.
  • The FEV1/FVC predicted ratio is population based and calculated independently from the FEV1 and FVC. Therefore, there may be minor differences from the predicted value shown here, and the value calculated from simple division of the predicted FEV1 by predicted FVC.
  • The Z-score is the number of standard deviations a value is from the predicted (mean) value. A negative Z-score represents data below the mean and a positive Z-score represents data above the mean.
  • Lower and upper limits of normal pulmonary function tests are calculated using the Z-score equation where Z-score is set to -1.645 for the LLN (5th percentile), and 1.645 for ULN (95th percentile).
  • PFT: pulmonary function test; GLI: Global Lung Function Initiative; FEV1: forced expiratory volume at 1 second; FVC: forced vital capacity; LLN: lower limit of normal; ULN: upper limit of normal.
