=== Introduction to Symptoms of Neurologic Disorders (Merck Manual Professional) ===
Section: Neurology > Symptoms of Neurologic Disorders
Source: https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/neurologic-disorders/symptoms-of-neurologic-disorders/introduction-to-symptoms-of-neurologic-disorders
Retrieved: 2026-09-25

Neurologic symptoms vary greatly because the nervous system controls many different body functions. Symptoms can include all forms of pain and can involve muscle function, sensation, interpretation of sensory stimuli, the special senses (vision, taste, smell, and hearing), sleep, awareness (consciousness), and mental function (cognition). The following are some examples:

Ageusia

Agnosia

Amnesia

Anosmia

Aphasia

Apraxia

Ataxia

Auras (eg, visual, olfactory), as occur in seizures, migraines, or encephalitis

Bradykinesia

Delirium

Dementia

Dysarthria (eg, due to stroke or cranial nerve palsies)

Dysgeusia

Dysphagia

Dystonia

Excessive daytime sleepiness

Headache

Insomnia

Involuntary movements (eg, chorea, myoclonus, tics)

Memory loss

Muscle cramps

Muscle stiffness, spasticity, or rigidity (as occurs in Parkinson disease, amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS), multiple sclerosis (MS), or stiff-person syndrome)

Numbness or other sensory deficits

Obtundation or coma

Seizures

Snoring

Tremor

Vertigo

Vision loss, blurring, scotomata (as occur with migraines), or diplopia

Weakness
