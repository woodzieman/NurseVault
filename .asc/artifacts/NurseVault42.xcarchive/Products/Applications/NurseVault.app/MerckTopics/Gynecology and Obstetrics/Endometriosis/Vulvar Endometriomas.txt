=== Vulvar Endometriomas (Merck Manual Professional) ===
Section: Gynecology and Obstetrics > Endometriosis

Retrieved: 2026-09-25

Rarely, endometriosis occurs in the vulva (or the vagina [1]), sometimes producing cysts (endometriomas), often at the site of previous surgery or a wound (eg, episiotomy, laceration during childbirth).

Endometriomas usually develop in the midline. They may be painful, particularly during intercourse. During menstruation, pain increases and endometriomas may enlarge. Endometriomas are tender and may appear blue. They can rupture, causing severe pain.

Diagnosis of vulvar endometriomas is by pelvic examination and biopsy.

Treatment of vulvar endometriomas involves excision.
