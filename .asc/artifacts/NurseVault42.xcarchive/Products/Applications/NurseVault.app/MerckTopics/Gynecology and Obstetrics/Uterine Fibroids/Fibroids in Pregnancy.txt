=== Fibroids in Pregnancy (Merck Manual Professional) ===
Section: Gynecology and Obstetrics > Uterine Fibroids
Source: https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/gynecology-and-obstetrics/uterine-fibroids/fibroids-in-pregnancy
Retrieved: 2026-09-25

Fibroids in pregnancy usually remain asymptomatic, but may present with pain from fibroid degeneration. Fibroids may increase risk of (1)

Preterm labor

Abnormal fetal presentation

Placenta previa and placental abruption

Early pregnancy loss or miscarriage

Postpartum hemorrhage

Rarely, fibroids partially obstruct the vagina.

Preconception evaluation is recommended for women who have very large fibroids or who have fibroids and have had a spontaneous abortion.
