=== Posttraumatic Iridocyclitis (Merck Manual Professional) ===
Section: Injuries; Poisoning > Eye Trauma
Source: https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/injuries-poisoning/eye-trauma/posttraumatic-iridocyclitis
Retrieved: 2026-09-25

(See also Overview of Eye Trauma.)

Symptoms of posttraumatic iridocyclitis include tearing, throbbing ache and redness of the eye, photophobia, and blurred vision. The pupil may be dilated or irregular in shape.

Diagnosis is by history, symptoms, and slit-lamp examination, which typically reveals flare (due to an increase in protein content of the aqueous humor from the inflammatory exudate) and white blood cells in the anterior chamber.

Treatment of posttraumatic iridocyclitis involves a cycloplegic (usually scopolamine 0.25% or homatropine 5%) and topical corticosteroids (eg, prednisolone acetate 1%).
