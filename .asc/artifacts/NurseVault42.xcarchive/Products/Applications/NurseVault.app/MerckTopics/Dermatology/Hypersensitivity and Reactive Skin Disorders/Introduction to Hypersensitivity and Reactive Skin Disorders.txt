The immune system plays a significant role in most skin disorders. The following skin disorders can be primarily characterized by a hypersensitivity or other type of reaction, be it to a medication, infection, or cancer:

Acute febrile neutrophilic dermatosis

Drug eruptions and reactions

Erythema multiforme

Erythema nodosum

Granuloma annulare

Panniculitis

Pyoderma gangrenosum

Stevens-Johnson syndrome

Toxic epidermal necrolysis
