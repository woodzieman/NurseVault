=== Manganese Deficiency (Merck Manual Professional) ===
Section: Nutrition > Mineral Deficiency and Toxicity
Source: https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/nutritional-disorders/mineral-deficiency-and-toxicity/manganese-deficiency
Retrieved: 2026-09-25

Manganese (Mn), necessary for healthy bone structure, is a component of several enzyme systems, including manganese-specific glycosyltransferases and phosphoenolpyruvate carboxykinase. Median intake is between 1.6 and 2.3 mg/day; absorption is 5 to 10%.

Manganese deficiency has not been conclusively documented. In an experimental study, severe manganese restriction led to dermatitis and hypocholesterolemia (1).
