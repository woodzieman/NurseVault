=== Introduction to Growth and Development (Merck Manual Professional) ===
Section: Pediatrics > Growth and Development
Source: https://www.merckmanuals.com/professional/pediatrics/growth-and-development/introduction-to-growth-and-development
Retrieved: 2026-09-25

Physical growth is an increase in size. Development is growth in function and capability. Both processes depend on genetic, nutritional, and environmental factors.

When describing physical and emotional development in children, it is useful to define certain age-based groups (1):

Neonate (newborn): Birth to 1 month

Infant: 1 month to 1 year

Young child: 1 year through 4 years

Older child: 5 years through 10 years

Adolescent: 11 years through 21 years
