    General Information
    Monitor renal function in all patients who receive lutetium Lu 177 vipivotide tetraxetan as treatment can cause nephrotoxicity. Description
  Text
  Lutetium Lu 177 vipivotide tetraxetan is a radioligand therapeutic agent indicated for the treatment of prostate-specific membrane antigen (PSMA)-positive metastatic prostate cancer that is androgen pathway modulation-naive or -sensitive (mAPMN/S), in combination with androgen receptor pathway inhibitor (ARPI) therapy, and androgen pathway modulation resistant in patients who are appropriate to delay taxane-based chemotherapy or who have previously received taxane-based chemotherapy. Take steps to minimize radiation exposure during and after treatment consistent with institutional good radiation safety practices and patient treatment procedures. Ensure patients increase oral fluid intake and advise them to void as often as possible to reduce bladder radiation. Lutetium Lu 177 vipivotide tetraxetan was FDA approved in 2022.

Mechanism Of Action
  Text
  Lutetium Lu 177 vipivotide tetraxetan is a radioligand therapeutic agent. The active moiety is the radionuclide lutetium-177 which is linked to a prostate-specific membrane antigen (PSMA)-binding ligand. PSMA is a transmembrane protein that is expressed in prostate cancer. After binding, the beta-minus emission from lutetium 177 delivers radiation to PSMA-expressing cells as well as to surrounding cells, inducing DNA damage and leading to cell death.

    Renal Impairment
    Description
    The AUC of lutetium Lu 177 vipivotide tetraxetan increased with decreasing CrCl. The effect of a baseline CrCl less than 54 mL/min on the pharmacokinetics of lutetium Lu 177 vipivotide tetraxetan has not been studied.   Text
  Lutetium Lu 177 vipivotide tetraxetan is administered intravenously. Vipivotide tetraxetan and nonradioactive lutetium vipivotide tetraxetan are 60% to 70% bound to human plasma proteins. The geometric mean volume of distribution of Lutetium Lu 177 vipivotide tetraxetan is 123 liters (CV, 78.1%); within 2.5 hours of administration, it is distributed to the GI tract, liver, lungs, kidneys, heart wall, bone marrow, and salivary glands. The lutetium Lu 177 vipivotide tetraxetan terminal elimination half-life is 41.6 hours (CV, 68.8%) and the clearance is 2.04 liters/hour (CV, 31.5%). It is primarily eliminated renally. Lutetium 177 decays to a stable hafnium 177 with a physical half-life of 6.647 days by emitting beta-minus radiation with a maximum energy of 0.498 MeV (CV, 79%) and photonic radiation (gamma) of 0.208 MeV (CV, 11%) and 0.113 MeV (CV, 6.4%).

Hazardous Drug Classification
Lutetium Lu 177 vipivotide tetraxetan is a radiopharmaceutical. Handle with appropriate safety measures to minimize radiation exposure consistent with institutional good radiation safety practices and Nuclear Regulatory Commission patient-release guidance.
Use waterproof gloves and effective radiation shielding when handling lutetium Lu 177 vipivotide tetraxetan.
Lutetium Lu 177 vipivotide tetraxetan should be used by or under the control of healthcare providers who are qualified by specific training and experience in the safe use and handling of radiopharmaceuticals, and whose experience and training have been approved by the appropriate governmental agency authorized to license the use of radiopharmaceuticals.
Patient Counseling
Educate patients to increase oral fluid intake and void as often as possible to decrease the potential for radiation to the bladder.
Advise patients to limit close contact (less than 3 feet) with most others for 2 days. Patients should limit close contact with children and pregnant women for 7 days.
Advise patients to abstain from sexual activity for 7 days.
Patients should sleep in a separate room from other people for 3 days unless they share a room with children (7 days) or a pregnant person (15 days).

    Administration Details
      Name
      Intravenous Administration
      Description
      Administer lutetium Lu 177 vipivotide tetraxetan as an IV injection using the syringe method, as an IV infusion using the gravity method, or as an IV infusion using the peristaltic pump method.
The syringe method or the peristaltic pump method of administration is preferred when administering a reduced dose of lutetium Lu 177 vipivotide tetraxetan.
Use aseptic technique and radiation shielding during handling and administration. Use tongs as needed when handling the vial(s) to minimize radiation exposure.
Do not inject lutetium Lu 177 vipivotide tetraxetan directly into any other intravenous solution.
Confirm the amount of radioactivity delivered to the patient with an appropriately calibrated dose calibrator prior to and after each administration.
Prior to administration, flush the IV catheter used exclusively for lutetium Lu 177 vipivotide tetraxetan administration with at least 10 mL of 0.9% Sodium Chloride Injection to ensure patency and minimize the risk of extravasation.
 
Intravenous Infusion, Syringe Method
Fit a disposable syringe with a syringe shield and a disposable sterile 9 cm, 18-gauge (long) needle.
Withdraw an appropriate volume of lutetium Lu 177 vipivotide tetraxetan to deliver the desired radioactivity. To aid the withdrawal of solution, a filtered 2.5 cm, 20-gauge needle (short venting needle) can be used to reduce resistance from the pressurized vial. Intravenous Infusion, Gravity Method
NOTE: If using the gravity method for a reduced dose, adjust the lutetium Lu 177 vipivotide tetraxetan dose before administration to avoid delivery of an incorrect volume of lutetium Lu 177 vipivotide tetraxetan.
Insert a 2.5 cm, 20-gauge (short) needle into the vial of lutetium Lu 177 vipivotide tetraxetan. Lutetium Lu 177 vipivotide tetraxetan will be infused directly from the original container.
Ensure the needle does not touch the lutetium Lu 177 vipivotide tetraxetan solution in the vial; do NOT connect the needle directly to the patient.
Connect the vial to 500 mL of 0.9% Sodium Chloride Injection via a catheter.
Do not allow the 0.9% Sodium Chloride Injection to flow into the lutetium Lu 177 vipivotide tetraxetan vial prior to initiation of the infusion and do not inject lutetium Lu 177 vipivotide tetraxetan directly into the 0.9% Sodium Chloride Injection solution.
Insert a second 9 cm, 18-gauge (long) needle into the lutetium Lu 177 vipivotide tetraxetan vial, ensuring that this needle touches and is secured to the bottom of the vial during the entire infusion.
Connect the long needle to the patient by an IV catheter that is primed with 0.9% Sodium Chloride Injection that is used exclusively for the lutetium Lu 177 vipivotide tetraxetan infusion.
Use a clamp or an infusion pump to regulate the flow of the 0.9% Sodium Chloride Injection via the short needle into the lutetium Lu 177 vipivotide tetraxetan vial.
Ensure that the level of solution in the lutetium Lu 177 vipivotide tetraxetan vial remains constant during the infusion.
Once the level of radioactivity is stable for at least 5 minutes, disconnect the vial from the long needle line and clamp the 0.9% Sodium Chloride Injection line. This should take approximately 30 minutes.
Flush the IV line with at least 10 mL of 0.9% Sodium Chloride Injection.
 
Intravenous Infusion, Peristaltic Pump Method
Insert a filtered 2.5 cm, 20-gauge needle (short venting needle) into the lutetium Lu 177 vipivotide tetraxetan vial. Lutetium Lu 177 vipivotide tetraxetan will be infused directly from its original container.
Ensure that the needle does not touch the lutetium Lu 177 vipivotide tetraxetan solution in the vial; do not connect this needle directly to the patient or to the peristaltic infusion pump.
Insert a second 9 cm, 18-gauge (long) needle into the lutetium Lu 177 vipivotide tetraxetan vial, ensuring that this needle touches and is secured to the bottom of the vial during the entire infusion.
Connect the long needle and a 0.9% Sodium Chloride Injection solution to a 3-way stopcock valve via appropriate tubing.
Connect the output of this valve to tubing on the input side of the peristaltic pump per the pump manufacturer's instructions.
Prime the line by opening the 3-way stopcock valve and pumping the lutetium Lu 177 vipivotide tetraxetan through the tubing until it reaches the exit of the valve.
Prime the IV catheter which will be connected to the patient by opening the 3-way stopcock valve to the 0.9% Sodium Chloride Injection solution and pumping it through until it exits the end of the catheter tubing.
Connect the primed IV catheter to the patient and set the 3-way stopcock valve such that the lutetium Lu 177 vipivotide tetraxetan solution is in line with the peristaltic pump.
Infuse an appropriate volume of lutetium Lu 177 vipivotide tetraxetan solution at approximately 25 mL/hour to deliver the desired radioactivity.
When the desired radioactivity has been delivered, stop the peristaltic pump and change the position of the 3-way stopcock valve so that the peristaltic pump is in line with the 0.9% Sodium Chloride Injection solution. Restart the peristaltic pump and infuse an IV flush of at least 10 mL of 0.9% Sodium Chloride Injection.

Dosage Adjustment Guidelines
  Hepatic Impairment
  Specific guidelines for dosage adjustments in hepatic impairment are not available; it appears that no dosage adjustments are needed.
  Renal Impairment
  Baseline Renal Impairment
Mild to moderate renal impairment (CrCl 30 to 89 mL/min): No dosage adjustment necessary.
Severe renal impairment or end-stage renal disease (CrCl less than 30 mL/min): The pharmacokinetics and safety of lutetium Lu 177 vipivotide tetraxetan have not been studied in this patient population.
 
Treatment-Related Nephrotoxicity
NOTE: CrCl recommendations are based on using the Cockroft-Gault equation and actual body weight.
Grade 2 or higher increase in SCr; OR CrCl less than 30 mL/min: Hold lutetium Lu 177 vipivotide tetraxetan until improvement.
A 40% or higher increase in SCr from baseline; AND 40% decrease in CrCl from baseline: Hold lutetium Lu 177 vipivotide tetraxetan for up to 4 weeks; if held for more than 4 weeks, discontinue therapy. Upon improvement or return to baseline, reduce the dose.
Grade 3 or higher nephrotoxicity: Permanently discontinue lutetium Lu 177 vipivotide tetraxetan.
