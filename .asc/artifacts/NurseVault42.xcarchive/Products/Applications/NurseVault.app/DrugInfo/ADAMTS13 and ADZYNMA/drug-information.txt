Hypersensitivity

This medication is contraindicated in patients with a history of hypersensitivity to it or any of its components.

pregnancy


The safety of rADAMTS13 during pregnancy has not been established. Limited data are insufficient to inform a medication associated risk of adverse developmental outcomes. There have been 4 patients exposed to rADAMTS13 during pregnancy. Two patients enrolled in clinical trials were found to be pregnant early in the first trimester while receiving prophylaxis with rADAMTS13. Both were removed from the trial due to study protocol. The first had no further exposure to rADAMTS13 and had a first trimester miscarriage approximately 2 months after study discontinuation. The investigator assessed the event as unrelated to rADAMTS13 use. The second patient resumed rADAMTS13 under a compassionate use program and delivered a healthy full-term infant with no safety concerns. Two additional patients were treated with rADAMTS13 in a compassionate use program during pregnancy. The first patient started rADAMTS13 treatment at 33 weeks gestation. A healthy neonate was delivered at 37 weeks with no safety concerns. The second patient started rADAMTS13 treatment during the second trimester. The neonate was delivered via cesarean section at week 29 and treating physician reported no adverse events due to rADAMTS13. No adverse maternal findings or treatment-related effects on embryo-fetal development were seen in female rats given dose of rADAMTS13 up to 10 times the human dose every third day from 2 weeks before mating through gestation day 16.


breast-feeding


There is no information regarding the presence of rADAMTS13 in human milk, the effects on the breast-fed child, or the effects on milk production. Consider the clinical need for treatment, as well as the developmental and health benefits of breast-feeding and any potential adverse effects to the breast-fed child.

Interactions

Adverse Reaction
abdominal pain, diarrhea, nausea, vomiting


Diarrhea (16.7%), abdominal pain (12.5%), nausea (12.5%), and vomiting (10.4%) were reported during clinical trials in patients receiving rADAMTS13.


dizziness, headache, infection, migraine


Headache (31.3%), migraine (14.6%), upper respiratory tract infection (12.5%), and dizziness (10.4%) were reported during clinical trials in patients receiving rADAMTS13.


antibody formation


Antibody formation may occur in patients receiving rADAMTS13. Neutralizing antibodies could potentially result in a decreased or lack of response to rADAMTS13. Neutralizing antibodies were not reported during clinical trials. All patients had been previously exposed to ADAMTS13 through plasma-based products. There is no data on immunogenicity with rADAMTS13 in previously untreated patients naive to plasma-based products. Patients may develop antibodies to host cell proteins which could potentially result in adverse reactions. There are no data on immunogenicity to host cell proteins in previously untreated patients. Neutralizing antibodies to ADAMTS13, including 1 death, have been reported with postmarketing use of rADAMTS13 in patients with congenital thrombotic thrombocytopenic purpura (cTTP). The reported death in a pediatric patient appears to be related to rADAMTS13. Prior to treatment, the patient had severe allergic reactions to fresh frozen plasma (FFP). The patient presented with neurologic symptoms which progressed, and the presence of neutralizing antibodies was identified approximately 10 months after starting prophylactic treatment with rADAMTS13. Current assays are unable to distinguish neutralizing antibodies to rADAMTS13 from neutralizing antibodies to endogenous ADAMTS13.


anaphylactoid reactions


Allergic-type hypersensitivity reactions, including anaphylactoid reactions, may occur in patients receiving rADAMTS13. Inform patients of early signs of hypersensitivity reactions, including tachycardia, chest tightness, wheezing, acute respiratory distress, hypotension, urticaria, pruritus, rhinoconjunctivitis, angioedema, lethargy, nausea, vomiting, paresthesia, or restlessness. Immediately discontinue rADAMTS13 if signs and symptoms of a severe hypersensitivity reaction occur and institute appropriate therapy.


Description

Recombinant a disintegrin and metalloproteinase with thrombospondin motifs 13 (rADAMTS13) is a recombinant form of endogenous ADAMTS13 indicated for intravenous enzyme replacement therapy in patients with congenital thrombotic thrombocytopenic purpura (cTTP). The efficacy of rADAMTS13 for prophylactic cTTP treatment was evaluated in a 12-month randomized crossover trial in which 46 patients (age range: 3 to 38 years) received rADAMTS13 or plasma-based therapy. Thirty-five patients continued on to a single arm 6-month period with rADAMTS13. No acute TTP events and 2 subacute TTP events were reported in rADAMTS13-treated patients compared to 1 acute and 5 subacute TTP events in those treated with plasma-based therapies. The mean annualized event rate for thrombocytopenia and microangiopathic hemolytic anemia manifestations was 2 and 0.38, respectively, in those receiving rADAMTS13 compared to 4.44 and 1.47, respectively, in those receiving plasma-based therapies. Five adult patients enrolled in the on-demand cohort and had a total of 6 acute TTP events. All 6 events resolved after treatment with rADAMTS13 (n = 2) or plasma-based therapies (n = 3). Recombinant ADAMTS13 has a favorable safety profile. The most common adverse reactions reported include headache, diarrhea, migraine, abdominal pain, and nausea.

Mechanism Of Action

Recombinant a disintegrin and metalloproteinase with thrombospondin motifs 13 (rADAMTS13) is a recombinant form of endogenous ADAMTS13. ADAMTS13 is a plasma zinc metalloprotease that regulates von Willebrand factor (vWF) activity by cleaving large and ultra large vWF multimers to smaller units. This reduces the platelet binding properties of vWF and its tendency to form microthrombi.

Pharmacokinetics

Recombinant ADAMTS13 is administered intravenously. At a dose of 40 International Units/kg the mean half-life and mean residence time (MRT) in adults and adolescents was 47.8 hours and 63.8 hours, respectively.
Both vWF antigen and vWF:ristocetin cofactor activity transiently decreased for 1 to 2 days with a 15% to 25% change from baseline after rADAMTS13 administration at the recommended dose.
 
Affected cytochrome P450 isoenzymes and drug transporters: none

Route-Specific Pharmacokinetics
Intravenous Route

Dose-related increases in ADAMTS13 activity were observed and reached a maximum within 1 hour of infusion during pharmacokinetic trials. After administration of a 40 International Units/kg dose, Cmax and AUC were 1.15 International Units/mL and 57.6 International Units x hour/mL, respectively, in adults and adolescents. The average ADAMTS13 activity (0 to 168 hours dosing interval) was 0.3 International Units/mL. The duration of activity above 10% was 5.8 days. A 40 International Unit/kg dose resulted in approximately 4- to 5-fold higher ADAMTS13 exposures and lower intersubject variability when compared to plasma-based therapies. Mean time duration above 10% ADAMTS13 activity and mean average ADAMTS13 activity were both approximately 3- to 4-fold higher after rADAMTS13 compared to plasma-based therapies.

Renal Impairment

Pediatrics

Population pharmacokinetic analysis suggests comparable ADAMTS13 activity exposures in children, adolescents, and adults. When dosed every other week, Cmax and AUC were 1.02 International Units/mL and 54 International Units x hour/mL, respectively, in patients younger than 12 years. The average ADAMTS13 activity was 0.16 International Units/mL. The duration of activity above 10% was 6.8 days. ADAMTS13 antigen and activity pharmacokinetic characteristics (MRT, volume at steady-state, clearance) are similar across age groups.

Injectable Administration


Visually inspect parenteral products for particulate matter and discoloration prior to administration whenever solution and container permit. Reconstituted rADAMTS13 solution should be clear and colorless.
Each vial is labeled with the actual rADAMTS12 activity, measured in terms of its potency in International Units. Calculate the dose and volume using the patient's body weight and the actual potency (not the nominal potency) as printed on the vial.


Intravenous Administration

Reconstitution
Allow the vials of rADAMTS13 powder and diluent to reach room temperature before use.
Wear clean exam gloves for reconstitution and preparation.
Open BAXJECT II Hi-Flow device package without touching the inside. Do not remove it from the package.
Turn the package with the BAXJECT II Hi-Flow device upside down and place it over the top of the diluent vial. Press straight down until the clear plastic spike pierces through the diluent vial.
Remove the BAXJECT II Hi-Flow device package. Do not remove the blue cap from the BAXJECT II Hi-Flow device. Do not touch the exposed purple plastic spike.
Turn the system over so the diluent vial is on top.
Press the BAXJECT II Hi-Flow device straight down until the purple plastic spike pierces through the rADAMTS13 powder vial. The vacuum will draw the diluent into the powder vial. Some bubbles or foam may appear; this is normal. Wait until foam or bubbles dissipate before administration.
Swirl the connected vials gently and continuously until the powder is completely dissolved. Do not shake the vial.
If the dose requires more than 1 vial of rADAMTS13, repeat steps to reconstitute each vial. Use a different BAXJECT II Hi-Flow device to reconstitute each vial of rADAMTS13.
Preparation for Administration
Remove the blue cap from the BAXJECT II Hi-Flow device.
Attach a Luer-lock syringe. Do not inject air into the system.
Turn the system so the rADAMTS13 vial is on top. Draw the reconstituted solution into the syringe slowly.
The contents of multiple vials can be drawn into the same syringe if needed.
Disconnect the syringe and attach a suitable injection needle or an infusion set.
Storage: Use within 3 hours after reconstitution. Keep at room temperature not to exceed 30 degrees C (86 degree F). Discard product not used within 3 hours after reconstitution.
Intravenous Infusion
Infuse at a rate of 2 to 4 mL/minute.
Do not administer rADAMTS13 in the same tubing or container at the same time with other medicinal products for infusion.
