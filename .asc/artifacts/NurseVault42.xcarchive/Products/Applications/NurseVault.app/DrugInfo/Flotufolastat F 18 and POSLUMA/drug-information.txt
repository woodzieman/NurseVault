    General Information
    General Information
    Image interpretation errors can occur with flotufolastat F 18 positron emission tomography (PET). A negative image does not rule out the presence of prostate cancer and a positive image does not confirm the presence of prostate cancer. The performance of flotufolastat F 18 for imaging metastatic pelvic lymph nodes in individuals prior to initial definitive therapy seems to be affected by serum prostate-specific antigen (PSA) concentrations and risk grouping. The performance of flotufolastat for imaging individuals with biochemical evidence of recurrence of prostate cancer seems to be affected by serum PSA concentrations. Flotufolastat F 18 uptake is not specific for prostate cancer and may occur in other types of cancer, in non-malignant processes, and in normal tissues. Clinical correlation, which may include histopathological evaluation, is recommended. The interpretation of flotufolastat F 18 PET may differ depending on imaging readers, particularly in the prostate/prostate bed region. Because of the associated risk of false positive interpretation, consider multidisciplinary consultation and histopathological confirmation when clinical decision-making hinges on flotufolastat F18 uptake only in the prostate/prostate bed region or only on uptake interpreted as borderline.

Description
  Text
  Flotufolastat F 18 is an intravenous, radioactive diagnostic agent indicated for positron emission tomography (PET) of prostate-specific membrane antigen (PSMA) positive lesions in persons with prostate cancer with suspected metastasis who are candidates for initial definitive therapy or with suspected recurrence based on elevated serum prostate-specific antigen (PSA) concentration. PET images obtained using flotufolastat F 18 indicate the presence of PSMA in tissues. Flotufolastat F 18 uptake is not specific for prostate cancer and may occur in other types of cancer, in non-malignant processes, and in normal tissues. Clinical correlation, which may include histopathological evaluation, is recommended.

Mechanism Of Action
  Text
  Flotufolastat F 18 binds to prostate-specific membrane antigen (PSMA) (IC50 = 4.4 nM) expressed on cells, including prostate cancer cells, and is internalized. Prostate cancer cells usually overexpress PSMA. Fluorine-18 is a positive beta particle-emitting radionuclide that can be detected using positron emission tomography (PET). PET images obtained using flotufolastat F 18 indicate the presence of PSMA in tissues. Lesions should be considered suspicious if uptake is greater than physiologic uptake in that tissue or greater than adjacent background if no physiologic uptake is expected. Tumors that do not express PSMA will not be visualized. Increased uptake in tumors is not specific for prostate cancer.

    Description
    The amount of radiation absorbed after exposure to a 296 MBq (8 mCi) dose of flotufolastat F 18 is 4.1 mSv. A positron emission tomography (PET) or computerized tomography (CT) scan will increase radiation exposure by an amount dependent on the settings used in the CT acquisition.   Text
  Flotufolastat F 18 is administered intravenously. Flotufolastat F 18 distributes to liver (15.8% of administered activity), heart blood pool (7.4%), and kidneys (3.2%) and is cleared from the blood. Flotufolastat F 18 does not undergo metabolism up to 50 minutes after administration. Elimination of flotufolastat F 18 is by urinary excretion. Approximately 7% of the administered activity was excreted in the urine in the first 2 hours post-injection, with approximately 15% excreted by 4.5 hours post-injection.
