        2 x 10^6 mesenchymal stromal cell/kg (MSC/kg) IV twice a week for 4 weeks. Administer infusion at least 3 days apart. Monitor for a response at 28 days. Description
  Text
  Remestemcel-L is an allogeneic (donor) bone marrow-derived mesenchymal stromal cell therapy. It is indicated for the treatment of steroid-refractory acute graft-versus-host disease (GVHD). Remestemcel-L is the first mesenchymal stromal cell therapy approved in the United States. It is thought to have immunomodulatory effects that inhibit T cell activation. In clinical trials, the overall response rate in pediatric patients at Day 28 was 70% (95% CI: 56.4, 82). This includes a complete response rate of 30% (95% CI: 18, 43.6) and a partial response rate of 41% (95% CI: 27.6, 55). The most common reported adverse reactions are viral and bacterial infections, fever, edema, abdominal pain, and hypertension. Remestemcel-L was FDA-approved in December 2024.

Mechanism Of Action
  Text
  The mechanism of action of remestemcel-L is unknown. It is thought to be related to immunomodulatory effects. In vitro studies show that mesenchymal stromal cells inhibit T cell activation as measured by proliferation and secretion of pro-inflammatory cytokines. Acute graft-versus-host disease (GVHD) occurs when alloreactive donor-derived T cells within the donated tissue (graft) trigger an immunological response. These alloreactive donor-derived T cells play a role in mediating systemic inflammation, cytotoxicity, and potential end organ damage associated with acute GVHD.

Pharmacodynamics
In pediatric patients with steroid resistant acute graft-versus-host disease (n = 40; age: 0.6 to 17 years), baseline tumor necrosis factor receptor type I (TNFR1) and suppressor of tumorigenicity 2 (ST2) were elevated and consistent with acute graft-versus-host disease. At Day 180, treatment with remestemcel-L reduced TNFR1 and ST2 concentrations by 79% and 75%, respectively, compared to baseline. Circulating concentrations of CD3+CD4+CD25+HLA-DR+ T cells (which represent activated T cells) were reduced by 64% at Day 180 after treatment with remestemcel-L as compared to baseline values.

Preparation
Prepare in a Biological Safety Cabinet (BSC).
Prepare a sterile water bath to a minimum depth of 4 inches. Warm water to 37 degrees C (+/- 2 degrees C) at least 30 minutes prior to thawing.
Preparation of Plasma-Lyte A
Plasma-Lyte 148 (pH 7.4 with no glucose) may be used as a substitute for Plasma-Lyte A.
Insert the Interlink blood bag spike into the Plasma-Lyte A bag.
Attach the threaded lock cannula to a 60 mL syringe.
Scrub the membrane of the Plasma-Lyte A bag interlink spike injection site with an alcohol wipe.
Attach the threaded lock cannula/syringe assembly to the Plasma-Lyte A bag to the injection site.
Measure and withdraw 40 mL of Plasma-Lyte A from the bag. Set aside for later use. Do not remove the syringe containing Plasma-Lyte A from the Plasma-Lyte A bag.
Preparation of remestemcel-L
Remove vial(s) from cryo-storage.
Place the vial(s) into an airtight zip sealed plastic bag(s). Use a separate zip sealed plastic bag for each vial of remestemcel-L.
Immerse the closed bag(s) in the water bath. Maintain the top closure above the water line. Thaw a maximum of 4 vials in the water bath at the same time.
Gently agitate the sealed zip seal bag(s) with the vials for approximately 5 to 8 minutes to thaw.
Remove the vials from the water bath prior to the last visible crystal of ice melting.
Do not exceed 15 minutes for each set of 4 vials.
Inspect the vial(s) while still in the bag(s) to identify there is no leakage of vial contents.
Remove the vial(s) from the sealed plastic bags.
Inspect the vial for foreign particular matter (FPM). If FPM is observed, do not use. Retain the vial and call Mesoblast at 844-889-6376.
Wipe the vial(s) and top cap with 70% alcohol. Wipe off any excess liquid.
Withdraw the required amount of remestemcel-L using a separate 18-gauge needle and syringe per vial.
Remove the needle from the syringe and attach the threaded lock cannula to the syringe.
Retrieve the infusion bag.
Wipe the membrane of the infusion bag interlink spike injection site with a sterile alcohol wipe.
Insert 1 interlink blood bag spike into the outermost port on the infusion bag (leave the middle port for the infusion line).
Attach the syringe to the spike and transfer the remestemcel-L into the infusion bag.
Remove the syringe with the threaded lock cannula and discard the syringe and the threaded lock cannula.
Repeat for each syringe until the required volume of remestemcel-L is added to the infusion bag.
Transfer of Plasma-Lyte A into the infusion bag
Retrieve the bag and 60 mL syringe containing Plasma-Lyte A.
Verify the syringe contains 40 mL of Plasma-Lyte A.
Aseptically remove the 60 mL syringe from the bag.
Use alcohol wipes to clean the membrane of the infusion bag interlink spike injection site.
Aseptically attach the syringe containing the Plasma-Lyte A to the spike.
Transfer the 40 mL of Plasma-Lyte A to the infusion bag.
Gently mix cells with Plasma-Lyte A.
Remove and discard the syringe.
 
Administration
Administer using an infusion pump.
Use a blood filter with a pore size of 40 to 260 microns.
Flush lines per institutional practice/policy for cellular infusions.
For patients weighing 35 kg or more, infuse at a rate of no more than 6 mL/minute.
For patients weighing less than 35 kg, infuse over the course of 60 minutes.
Storage: Infusion must be given within 5 hours from the start time of the first vial(s) of remestemcel-L thaw. Follow institutional guidelines for disposal of biohazard materials.
