          Dosage Description
          7.5 X 109 to 72 X 109 viable cells as a single IV dose. Administer lifileucel at 24 hours (but no later than 4 days) after the last dose of lymphodepleting chemotherapy consisting of cyclophosphamide 60 mg/kg IV (with mesna) once daily for 2 days followed by fludarabine 25 mg/m2 IV once daily for 5 days. Premedicate patients with acetaminophen and an H1 antihistamine (e.g., diphenhydramine) approximately 30 to 60 minutes prior to the lifileucel infusion. Beginning 1 day after the lifileucel infusion, give filgrastim (or a biosimilar product) once daily until the absolute neutrophil count is greater than 1,000 cells/mm3 for 3 consecutive days, or per institutional standards. At 3 to 24 hours after the lifileucel infusion, start aldesleukin 600,000 international units/kg IV every 8 to 12 hours for up to a maximum of 6 doses to support cell expansion. In a pooled analysis of a multi-cohort, phase 2 (C-144-01) trial, treatment with a single dose of lifileucel (median of 21.1 X 109 cells) resulted in an independent review committee-assessed objective response rate of 31.4% (complete response rate, 5.2%) in 153 patients with unresectable or metastatic (stage IIIC or IV) melanoma who were either primary or secondary refractory to prior anti-programmed death (PD)-1/PD ligand-1 (PD-L1) therapy. At a median follow-up time of 27.6 months, the median duration of response was not reached (range, 1.4 to more than 45 months), the median progression-free survival time was 4.1 months, and the median overall survival time was 13.9 months. Patients (median age, 56 years; range, 20 to 79 years) in this analysis had received a median of 3 (range, 1 to 9) prior therapies; 39 of 41 patients with BRAF V600-mutated disease received a BRAF inhibitor with or without a MEK inhibitor.

    Headline
    mortality
    General Information
    Treatment-related mortality occurred in patients during the lymphodepleting period and following lifileucel administration. Adverse reactions linked to these deaths include severe infectious events (i.e., sepsis, pneumonia, encephalitis), internal organ hemorrhage (i.e., abdominal and intracranial hemorrhage), acute renal failure, acute respiratory failure, cardiac arrhythmias, hepatotoxicity, and bone marrow failure.
    Headline
    prolonged and/or recurrent cytopenias
    General Information
    Prolonged and/or recurrent cytopenias (e.g., thrombocytopenia, lymphopenia, neutropenia, leukopenia, pancytopenia) have been reported following lifileucel administration. Administer filgrastim (or a biosimilar product) beginning 1 day after lifileucel; continue daily until the absolute neutrophil count is greater than 1,000 cells/mm3 for 3 consecutive days or per institutional guidelines. Monitor complete blood counts post lifileucel infusion. Avoid using anticoagulants in patients with persistent or repeated thrombocytopenia; monitor closely if anticoagulant therapy is necessary in these patients.
    Headline
    infection
    General Information
    Do not administer lifileucel in patients with a clinically significant systemic infection. Severe, life-threatening, and fatal infections have been reported with lifileucel therapy. Consider screening patients for recent or active infections or other inflammatory conditions, including hepatitis B or C, HIV, and cytomegalovirus. Monitor patients for signs and symptoms of infection prior to and after the lifileucel infusion; administer prophylactic antimicrobial therapy per institutional guidelines.
    Headline
    cardiac disease, cardiopulmonary disease, hypotension, pulmonary disease
    General Information
    Use lifileucel with caution in patients with a history of cardiopulmonary disease or hypotension. Fatal cardiopulmonary reaction (i.e., cardiac arrhythmias, acute respiratory failure) has occurred in patients receiving lifileucel; administration requires an inpatient hospital setting with an intensive care facility and specialists skilled in cardiopulmonary or intensive care medicine available. Monitor all patients for cardiopulmonary reactions during the treatment course; monitor patients with signs and symptoms of cardiac disease or pulmonary disease (e.g., respiratory failure) before and after the lifileucel infusion. Withhold or discontinue the lifileucel infusion if severe toxicity occurs or if the patient is ineligible to receive post-lifileucel aldesleukin (IL-2) therapy.
    Headline

    Advise patients of reproductive potential to avoid pregnancy during lifileucel treatment and to discuss becoming pregnant after lifileucel administration with their healthcare provider. There are no available data with lifileucel use in pregnant patients; animal reproductive and developmental toxicity studies have not been conducted. Report pregnancies to Iovance Biotherapeutics, Inc at 1-833-400-IOVA.
    Headline

    General Information
    Use lifileucel with caution in patients with preexisting renal impairment. Worsened renal impairment and fatal renal toxicity (i.e., acute renal failure) have been reported following lifileucel therapy. Monitor renal function in all patients during the treatment course; monitor patients with signs and symptoms of acute renal failure before and after the lifileucel infusion. Withhold or discontinue the lifileucel infusion if severe acute renal failure occurs or if the patient is ineligible to receive post-lifileucel aldesleukin (IL-2) therapy.

Description
  Text
  Lifileucel is a tumor-derived autologous T-cell immunotherapy indicated for the treatment of adult patients with unresectable or metastatic melanoma previously treated with a PD-1 blocking antibody, and if BRAF V600 mutation positive, a BRAF inhibitor with or without a MEK inhibitor. Lifileucel has a black box warning for treatment-related mortality, prolonged severe cytopenias, severe infection, and cardiopulmonary and renal impairment.

Mechanism Of Action
  Text
  Lifileucel is a tumor-derived autologous T-cell immunotherapy. It is manufactured by harvesting patient tumor tissue from 1 or more melanoma tumor lesions and then the T-cells derived from the tumor material are expanded in cell culture, washed, formulated as a cell suspension, and cryopreserved. The lifileucel suspension consists primarily of tumor-derived T-cells (CD4+T and CD8+T lineages) but may also contain monocytes, other lymphocytes (e.g., B cells and NK cells), and melanoma tumor cells from the original tumor tissue. After non-myeloablative lymphodepletion chemotherapy, the tumor-infiltrating lymphocytes (TIL) are infused back into the patient enabling preferential engraftment by TIL. IL-2 (aldesleukin) administration post TIL infusion promotes in vivo expansion of the infused T-cells.

Pharmacokinetics
  Text
  Lifileucel is administered intravenously. The proportion of T-cell receptor (TCR) clones composed of clonotypes identified in the lifileucel product increases from a pre-infusion mean of 16% (n = 125) to 83% at day 4 after the infusion. At day 14 post-infusion, TCR clones declined to 51% (n = 51) and remained at 37% to 41% from day 42 (n = 120) to month 12 (n = 37). There was no significant correlation between lifileucel persistence and efficacy.
Pharmacodynamics: Peak levels of IL-15 and CXCL10 were observed within 4 days of the lifileucel infusion; these levels returned to baseline within 1 to 3 months. INF-gamma dropped to below baseline within 4 days of the lifileucel infusion and returned to baseline by day 14. There was no other noticeable changes in other cytokines and chemokines (IL-6, IL-7, IL-9, IL-10, IL-12(p40), CCL2, TNF-alpha) levels. No difference was observed in chemokines and cytokine levels between responding and non-responding patients.

Administration
  General Information
  For storage information, see the specific product information within the How Supplied section.
NOTE: Follow universal and local biosafety guidelines applicable for the handling and disposal of lifileucel to avoid potential transmission of infectious diseases.
Premedication
Premedicate with acetaminophen and an H1-antihistamine (e.g., diphenhydramine) approximately 30 to 60 minutes prior to the infusion; avoid corticosteroids use except in the case of a life-threatening emergency.
Neutropenic Risk
Administer filgrastim (or a biosimilar product) beginning Day 1 after the infusion; continue daily until ANC if greater than 1,000 cells/mm3 for 3 consecutive days or per institutional standard.

    Administration Details
      Name
      Intravenous Administration
      Description
      Lifileucel is for autologous and intravenous use.
A single dose of lifileucel consists of 1 to 4 infusion bag(s); each 100-mL to 125-mL bag is contained within a protective metal cassette.
Verify aldesleukin is available prior to thawing lifileucel; additionally, ensure cardiopulmonary and intensive care medication and equipment are available prior to the infusion.
Coordinate the timing of the lifileucel thaw and infusion; confirm the infusion time in advance and adjust the start time for thaw so that the recipient will be ready.
Preparation
Match the patient's identity with the patient identifiers on the cassette(s) and infusion bag(s).
Thaw and infuse 1 bag at a time if more than 1 bag has been provided; wait to thaw the next bag until the previous bag has been safely and completely administered.
Remove the infusion bag from the cassette; place the infusion bag inside a second sterile and sealable bag in case of a leak and to protect ports from contamination.
Thaw the bag at approximately 35 to 39 degrees C using either a water bath or a dry thaw method until there is no visible ice or frozen contents; the total time from start to completion of thaw should be no more than 10 minutes.
Remove the bag from thawing device, remove the sealable plastic bag, and wipe dry; do not wash, spin down, or re-suspend lifileucel in a new media prior to infusion.
If cell clumps are visible after thawing, gently mix the contents of the bag by inverting the bag and gently massage the bag to disperse cell clumps.
Storage: If not used immediately, each thawed bag may be stored at room temperature (18 to 25 degrees C) for up to 3 hours; do not re-freeze or refrigerate thawed product.
Intravenous Infusion
Prior to the infusion, confirm the patient's health status is acceptable for lifileucel and subsequent aldesleukin administration.
Verify the patient's identity matches the patient identifiers on the infusion bag.
Prime the tubing with normal saline prior to the infusion.
Do not use a leukocyte depleting filter.
Administer the lifileucel infusion at a rate of approximately 1 mL/minute for the first 5 minutes, then increase the rate to 5 mL/minute to 10 mL/minute.
Infuse the entire contents of the infusion bag within 3 hours of thawing.
After all infusion bags have been administered to complete a single dose, rinse the tubing with normal saline at the same infusion rate to ensure all product is delivered.
Monitor patients for signs and symptoms of infusion-related or hypersensitivity reactions during and after the infusion.
