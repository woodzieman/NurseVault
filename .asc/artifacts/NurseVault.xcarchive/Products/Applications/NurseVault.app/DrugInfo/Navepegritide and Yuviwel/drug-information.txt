Description
  Text
  Navepegritide is a C-type natriuretic peptide (CNP) analog indicated to increase linear growth in individuals with achondroplasia with open epiphyses. In a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled clinical trial in 84 pediatric patients ages 2 to 12 years with achondroplasia, navepegritide efficacy was determined by the change from baseline in annualized growth velocity (AGV) after 52 weeks. At the end of the 52-week treatment period, the treatment difference in the change from baseline in AGV was 1.5 cm per year in participants treated with navepegritide compared to those receiving placebo. At the end of the study, 57 participants enrolled in an open-label extension. The improvement in AGV was maintained in participants who had 2 years of treatment with navepegritide. The most common adverse reactions were vomiting, injection-site reactions, pain in extremity, and nausea. Monitor for symptoms of decreased blood pressure, such as dizziness, fatigue, or nausea. Navepegritide was FDA approved in February 2026.

Mechanism Of Action
  Text
  Navepegritide is a human C-type natriuretic peptide (CNP) analog. Achondroplasia is caused by a gain-of-function variant in fibroblast growth factor receptor 3 (FGFR3), resulting in overactive downstream signaling that inhibits endochondral ossification and leads to short stature and skeletal dysplasia. Navepegritide is a prodrug of active CNP, consisting of a CNP moiety conjugated to two branched methoxy polyethylene glycol (mPEG) moieties. The CNP released from navepegritide has the same receptor binding affinity and activity as endogenous CNP. The released CNP binds to natriuretic peptide receptor-B (NPR-B), stimulating an increase in cyclic guanosine monophosphate (cGMP) and activation of protein kinase G, which inhibits the mitogen-activated protein kinase (MAPK) signaling pathway, thereby functionally antagonizing the overactive downstream signaling of FGFR3. Like endogenous CNP, navepegritide promotes chondrocyte differentiation and proliferation, thereby stimulating skeletal bone growth in individuals with achondroplasia.

Pharmacokinetics
  Route Specific Pharmacokinetics
    Name
    Subcutaneous Route
    Description
    In healthy adults and in individuals with achondroplasia, plasma concentrations of navepegritide and C-type natriuretic peptide (CNP) released from navepegritide increased proportionally within a dose range of 0.01 to 0.15 mg/kg. In individuals with achondroplasia administered navepegritide 0.1 mg/kg subcutaneously once weekly, the predicted steady state geometric mean Cmax and AUC of navepegritide over the weekly dosing interval were 1,360 nanograms/mL and 193,000 hour x nanograms/mL, respectively; for CNP released from navepegritide, the predicted geometric mean Cmax and AUC were 36 pmol/L and 4,410 hour x pmol/L, respectively. Steady state concentrations of navepegritide and CNP released from navepegritide were reached after approximately 3 once-weekly doses.

  Text
  Navepegritide is administered by subcutaneous injection. The model-derived geometric mean apparent volume of distribution was 1.8 L for navepegritide and 5.1 L for C-type natriuretic peptide (CNP) released from navepegritide. Navepegritide releases CNP via auto-cleavage of the proprietary TransCon Linker, following first-order kinetics and resulting in continuous systemic exposure to CNP over the weekly dosing interval. CNP is metabolized via natural degradation pathways for peptides, resulting in small peptide fragments and amino acids. In individuals with achondroplasia, the model-derived geometric mean steady state apparent clearance of navepegritide and CNP released from navepegritide were 0.052 L/day and 1,950 L/day, respectively. In individuals with achondroplasia receiving 0.1 mg/kg navepegritide once weekly, the predicted mean apparent elimination half-life of navepegritide and CNP released from navepegritide were 6.7 days and 5.3 days, respectively.

Administration
  General Information

  Route Specific Administrations
    Administration Details
      Name
      Subcutaneous Administration
      Description
      Navepegritide may be administered by caregivers after appropriate training on the preparation and administration.
Navepegritide is available in 3 different vial sizes; selection of the appropriate vial is based on the patient's actual body weight (kg).
Navepegritide must be reconstituted using the provided diluent syringe containing Sterile Water for Injection.
The injection volume is based on the patient's actual body weight and concentration of reconstituted navepegritide. The navepegritide reconstituted concentration of the 1.3 mg vial, after adding 0.8 mL reconstitution volume, is 2.2 mg/mL. The navepegritide reconstituted concentration of the 2.8 mg vial, after adding 0.8 mL reconstitution volume, is 4.6 mg/mL. The navepegritide reconstituted concentration of the 5.5 mg vial, after adding 1.1 mL reconstitution volume, is 5.5 mg/mL.
 
Reconstitution
Remove navepegritide vial and prefilled diluent syringe from the refrigerator and allow both to reach room temperature for approximately 30 minutes before reconstitution.
Screw a preparation needle onto the prefilled diluent syringe and inject the entire diluent volume into the vial.
Shake the vial up and down for 15 seconds; do not swirl or roll.
Allow the reconstituted navepegritide to stand at room temperature for 5 minutes after shaking.
 
Preparation
After reconstitution, screw a new preparation needle onto the injection syringe and withdraw the prescribed injection volume from the reconstituted vial.
Remove air from the withdrawn dose volume, and ensure the withdrawn dose volume is correct before continuing.
Remove and dispose the preparation needle from the injection syringe.
Screw the injection needle onto the injection syringe before administration.
 
Subcutaneous injection
Using the prepared syringe, administer the prescribed injection volume by injecting subcutaneously in the abdominal region (2 inches from the belly button) or thighs. Caregivers may also administer navepegritide subcutaneously in the buttocks or back of the upper arm. Do not inject into other sites.
Two kits are needed to achieve a complete dose where the prescribed injection volume is greater than 1 mL. If 2 kits are needed, give 1 injection after the other in different injection sites.
Rotate injection sites. Avoid injecting into red, swollen, or scarred skin.
Missed dose: Administer a missed dose as soon as possible but not more than 2 days after the missed dosing day. If more than 2 days have passed from the dosing day, skip the missed dose and administer the next dose on the next regularly scheduled dosing day. Navepegritide can be administered up to 2 days before or 2 days after the scheduled dosing day to avoid missed doses. Resume once weekly dosing for the next dose at the previously scheduled dosing day. When changing the regularly scheduled dosing day to a different day of the week, at least 5 days should elapse between doses.
Storage: After reconstitution, navepegritide solution can be stored at room temperature up to 86 degrees F (30 degrees C) for up to 4 hours. Discard any unused reconstituted solution.
