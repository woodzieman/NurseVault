Description
  Text
  Ichthammol is a topical drawing salve indicated for the treatment of insect bites and stings from mosquitoes, spider, and bees, plant irritations (such as nettles or poison ivy), and minor skin infections (such as an ingrown toenail) and for the removal of splinters and silvers. It is a homeopathic therapy purported to have antimicrobial, antiinflammatory, antipruriginous, and analgesic properties. Ichthammol is also known as sulfonated shade oil and has a pungent, sulfurous odor. Its use is associated with the staining of skin and clothing.

Mechanism Of Action
  Text
  Ichthammol influences the body's inflammatory response likely by impeding inflammatory cell function. In animals, ichthammol use was associated with dose-dependent inhibition of leukotriene B4 (LTB4) induced calcium mobilization and hydrogen peroxide generation; superoxide anion production by phorbol ester stimulation was also reduced. Diminished cell secretion and biologic inhibition of chemotactic factors and LTB4 were noted in chemotactic factor stimulated and unstimulated peripheral human leukocyte cultures. It has been suggested that ichthammol decreases LTB4 production in ionophore-stimulated human polymorphonuclear leukocytes (PMNLs) by inhibition of 5-hydroxyeicosatetraenoic acid (5-HETE) production from arachidonic acid.
