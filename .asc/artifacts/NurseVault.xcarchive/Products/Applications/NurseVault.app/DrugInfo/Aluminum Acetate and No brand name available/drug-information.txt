Indication Specific Dosing

For the treatment of minor skin irritation due to poison ivy, oak, or sumac, insect bites or stings, tinea pedis, or contact dermatitis caused by soaps, detergents, cosmetics, or jewelry

Topical dosage (compress or wet dressing)
Adults


1 to 3 packets diluted for compress or wet dressing of affected area as needed. Discontinue use if condition worsens or symptoms do not improve in 7 days.


Topical dosage (soak)
Adults



1 to 3 packets diluted for soaking affected area 3 times daily as needed. Discontinue use if condition worsens or symptoms do not improve in 7 days.

Description

Aluminum acetate is a topical astringent indicated for the temporary relief of minor skin irritations due to poison ivy, oak, or sumac, insect bites, athlete's foot, and contact dermatitis caused by soap, detergents, cosmetics, or jewelry. It is also known as Burow's Solution. The product is available in powder packets; aluminum acetate is produced from the reaction of calcium acetate and aluminum sulfate powders when mixed in water. Soaking skin too long with aluminum acetate can cause dryness in some skin conditions.

Mechanism Of Action

Aluminum acetate is an astringent and shrinks or constricts body tissues locally after topical application through osmotic flow of water (or other fluids) away from the area where applied. Externally applied astringents, which cause mild coagulation of skin proteins, dry, harden, and protect the skin.

Pharmacokinetics

Administration

For storage information, see the specific product information within the How Supplied section.

Topical Administration


For external use only. Not for ophthalmic administration; avoid contact with eyes.
Dilute powder packets before use. Mix 1 to 3 packets in water to obtain the following modified solution:
Mix 1 packet in 16 ounces of water to yield a 1:40 dilution (0.14% aluminum acetate).
Mix 2 packets in 16 ounces of water to yield a 1:20 dilution (0.28% aluminum acetate).
Mix 3 packets in 16 ounces of water to yield a 1:13 dilution (0.42% aluminum acetate).
Do not strain or filter the solution.
Storage: Discard solution after each use.


Other Topical Formulations

Topical compress or wet dressing
Soak a clean, soft cloth in the solution.
Apply cloth loosely to affected area for 15 to 30 minutes.
Do not cover compress or wet dressing in plastic to prevent evaporation.
 
Topical soak
Soak affected area for 15 to 30 minutes.
