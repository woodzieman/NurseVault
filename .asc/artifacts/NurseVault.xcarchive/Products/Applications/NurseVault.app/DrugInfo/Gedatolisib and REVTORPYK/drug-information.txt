          Heading

          Dosage Description
          180 mg IV on days 1, 8, and 15, every 28 days, in combination with fulvestrant (500 mg IM on cycle 1 days 1 and 15, then day 1 of subsequent 28-day cycles), with or without palbociclib (125 mg PO once daily on days 1 to 21, followed by 7 days off, every 28 days), until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity. Prophylactically administer a steroid-containing alcohol-free mouthwash 4 times daily for the first 8 weeks of gedatolisib therapy to prevent the incidence and severity of stomatitis; continue longer if needed. In the VIKTORIA-1 trial, all pre/perimenopausal women were required to receive a gonadotropin-releasing hormone (GnRH) agonist. Treatment with gedatolisib in combination with fulvestrant and palbociclib significantly improved median progression-free survival (PFS) compared with fulvestrant alone (9.3 months vs. 2 months) in patients with locally advanced (inoperable) or metastatic HR-positive, HER2-negative breast cancer without a detectable PIK3CA mutation in a multicenter, randomized, open-label clinical trial (Study 1 of VIKTORIA-1); treatment with gedatolisib plus fulvestrant (without palbociclib) also significantly improved PFS compared with fulvestrant alone (7.4 months vs. 2 months). The objective response rate was 32% in patients who received gedatolisib plus fulvestrant and palbociclib, 28% in patients treated with gedatolisib plus fulvestrant, and 1% in patients who received fulvestrant alone; the median duration of response was 17.5 months versus 12 months versus not evaluable, respectively. Overall survival data were not mature at the time of the PFS analysis; crossover from the fulvestrant arm to one of the gedatolisib arms was permitted at the time of disease progression. Patients in the VIKTORIA-1 study were required to have progression on or after treatment with a CDK4/6 inhibitor and a nonsteroidal aromatase inhibitor AI therapy, and could have received up to two prior lines of endocrine therapy for locally advanced (inoperable) or metastatic disease.

    Headline
    diabetes mellitus
    General Information
    The safety of gedatolisib in patients with Type 1 diabetes mellitus or uncontrolled Type 2 diabetes mellitus has not been established as these patients were excluded from Study 1 of the VIKTORIA-1 trial. Test fasting glucose levels and hemoglobin A1C at baseline and optimize fasting glucose; achieve optimal glucose control prior to starting each gedatolisib infusion. Initiate at-home glucose monitoring for patients with an A1C level greater than 6.4%. Consider consultation with a healthcare professional experienced in the treatment of hyperglycemia; patients with a history of well-controlled diabetes mellitus may require intensified antihyperglycemic treatment and close monitoring of fasting glucose levels. An interruption of therapy, dose reduction, or permanent discontinuation of therapy may be necessary if hyperglycemia occurs.
    Headline
    pregnancy
    General Information
    Avoid the use of gedatolisib during pregnancy. Gedatolisib can cause fetal harm based on its mechanism of action. There are no adequately controlled studies during human pregnancy. Inhibition of the PI3K and mTOR pathways has been associated with adverse embryo-fetal growth and lethality in animals, with PIK3CA knockout mice experiencing developmental delay and mortality, and mTOR knockout mice experiencing early lethality; in both animal models, early embryonic death was a result of impaired cell proliferation and structural development. Apprise individuals receiving gedatolisib of the potential hazard to the fetus.
    Headline
    people who can cause pregnancy in others, people who may become pregnant, reproductive risk
    General Information
    Counsel people who may become pregnant and people who can cause pregnancy in others about the reproductive risk associated with gedatolisib. Gedatolisib may cause fetal harm. Verify pregnancy status prior to treatment initiation. Advise people who may become pregnant to use effective contraception while taking gedatolisib and for 2 weeks after the last dose. For those that can cause pregnancy in others, contraception should be used during gedatolisib treatment and for 2 weeks after the last dose. Based on animal studies, gedatolisib may cause male and female infertility; these findings were reversible in animals.
    Headline
    breast-feeding
    General Information
    Advise patients to avoid breast-feeding while taking gedatolisib and for 2 weeks after the last dose. There are no data on the presence of gedatolisib or its metabolites in human milk, the effects on the breastfed child, or the effects on milk production.

    oral ulceration, stomatitis
    General Information
    Stomatitis, including oral ulceration and oral mucositis, occurred in 72% (grade 3, 22%) of patients treated with gedatolisib in combination with both fulvestrant and palbociclib and in 58% of patients who received gedatolisib plus fulvestrant (without palbociclib) (grade 3, 12%) in a randomized clinical trial, compared with 2.4% of patients who received fulvestrant alone. The median time to onset was 4 to 7 days. The incidence of grade 3 or higher stomatitis was higher in patients aged 65 years or older treated with gedatolisib in combination with fulvestrant and palbociclib compared with those younger than 65 (37% vs. 16%); the incidence was also higher in older patients who received gedatolisib plus fulvestrant, without palbociclib, versus younger patients (18% vs. 10%). Higher gedatolisib exposure was associated with an increased incidence of grade 3 or 4 stomatitis or mucositis. A steroid-containing alcohol-free mouthwash should be used as recommended for at least the first 8 weeks of therapy to reduce the incidence and severity of stomatitis. If stomatitis occurs, increase the frequency of the mouthwash and administer other topical treatments as clinically indicated. An interruption of therapy, dose reduction, or permanent discontinuation of therapy may be necessary if stomatitis occurs, depending on the severity.

Description
  Text
  Gedatolisib is a kinase inhibitor of class I PI3K isoforms (alpha, beta, delta, gamma) and mTOR complexes mTORC1 and mTORC2, resulting in downstream inhibition of multiple effectors, including AKT. It is FDA-approved for the treatment of hormone receptor-positive, HER2-negative locally advanced or metastatic breast cancer without a PIK3CA mutation following progression on or after treatment with at least one line of endocrine therapy in the metastatic setting, in combination with fulvestrant, with or without palbociclib. Severe hyperglycemia has been reported; fasting glucose levels and A1C should be tested at baseline, with optimal glucose control achieved prior to each gedatolisib infusion. The safety of gedatolisib in patients with Type 1 or uncontrolled Type 2 diabetes mellitus has not been established. Gedatolisib was FDA-approved in July 2026.

Mechanism Of Action
  Text
  Gedatolisib is a kinase inhibitor of class I PI3K isoforms (alpha, beta, delta, gamma) and mTOR complexes mTORC1 and mTORC2, resulting in downstream inhibition of multiple effectors, including AKT. In estrogen receptor (ER)-positive breast cancer cell lines and xenograft models harboring wild-type or mutant PIK3CA, gedatolisib induced apoptosis and antiproliferative effects in vitro and reduced tumor cell growth in vivo. In a human ER-positive breast cancer xenograft model harboring a PIK3CA mutation, the combination of gedatolisib with palbociclib and fulvestrant increased tumor growth inhibition compared to each treatment alone or the doublet combinations.

  Text
  Gedatolisib is administered intravenously. In vitro, gedatolisib is 98% bound to plasma proteins in a non-concentration dependent manner with a blood-to-plasma ratio of 0.87. The volume of distribution of gedatolisib is 393 L. Gedatolisib has an elimination half-life of 36 hours with an estimated clearance of 8 L/hour (CV, 23%). In vitro, gedatolisib is a P-gp and BCRP substrate. After a single radiolabeled dose to healthy subjects, approximately 67.4% was recovered in feces (67% unchanged) and 12.2% in urine (11.6% unchanged).

Hazardous Drug Classification
Approved by FDA after NIOSH 2024 list was published and meets criteria for NIOSH Table 2 Hazardous Drugs.
Observe and exercise appropriate precautions for handling, preparation, administration, and disposal of hazardous drugs.
Use double chemotherapy gloves and a protective gown. Prepare in a biological safety cabinet or compounding aseptic containment isolator with a closed system drug transfer device. Eye/face and respiratory protection may be needed during preparation and administration.
Emetic Risk
Moderate
Administer routine antiemetic prophylaxis prior to treatment.
Concomitant Medications
Prophylactically administer a steroid-containing alcohol-free mouthwash 4 times daily for the first 8 weeks of gedatolisib therapy to prevent the incidence and severity of stomatitis; continue longer if needed.
Patient Counseling
Patients should limit sun exposure during treatment with gedatolisib.

      Intravenous Administration
      Description
      If a dose is delayed or missed, administer as soon as possible; do not wait until the next planned dose in the cycle. Adjust the timing of subsequent dose to maintain the 3 weeks on followed by 1 week off schedule.
Do not administer other medications concomitantly via the same IV line.
Do not use chloride-containing infusion solutions (e.g., normal saline, Ringer's solution, Lactated Ringer's solution) for reconstitution or dilution.
Reconstitution
Add 20 mL of Sterile Water for Injection or 5% Dextrose Injection to the gedatolisib vial.
Swirl until completely dissolved (up to 5 minutes). Minor foaming and opalescence may be observed.
Storage after reconstitution: May be stored at room temperature (20 to 25 degrees C or 68 to 77 degrees F) for up to 4 hours. Do not freeze or refrigerate.
Dilution
Add the appropriate volume of reconstituted gedatolisib to a 250 mL bag of 5% Dextrose Injection.
Gently invert to thoroughly mix the solution. Do not shake.
Storage after dilution: Store at room temperature (20 to 25 degrees C or 68 to 77 degrees F) for up to 24 hours. Do not freeze or refrigerate.
IV Infusion
Flush the IV line with 5% Dextrose Injection prior to administration.
Administer using a 0.2 or 0.22-micron polyether sulfone filter.
Administer as an IV infusion over 30 minutes through a dedicated line.
Flush the IV line with 5% Dextrose Injection after completion of the infusion.
