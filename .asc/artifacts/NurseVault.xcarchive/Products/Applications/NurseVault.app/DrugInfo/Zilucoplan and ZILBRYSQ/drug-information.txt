    Headline
    meningococcal infection
    General Information
    Zilucoplan is contraindicated for initiation in individuals with unresolved serious Neisseria meningitidis infection. Complete or update meningococcal vaccination (for serogroups A, B, C, W, and Y) according to the Advisory Committee on Immunization Practices (ACIP) recommendations for individuals receiving a complement inhibitor at least 2 weeks prior to zilucoplan initiation. Revaccinate individuals in accordance with ACIP recommendations, considering the duration of zilucoplan treatment. ACIP recommendations for meningococcal vaccination administration in individuals receiving complement inhibitors differ from the administration schedule provided in the vaccine prescribing information. Administer meningococcal vaccine(s) as soon as possible according to ACIP recommendations and provide antibacterial drug prophylaxis if urgent treatment with zilucoplan is needed in an individual who is not up-to-date with vaccines for meningococcal serogroups A, B, C, W, and Y. Optimal dosage regimens for antibacterial drug prophylaxis and their efficacy have not been studied in unvaccinated or vaccinated individuals receiving complement inhibitors, including zilucoplan. Consider the benefits and risks of treatment with zilucoplan and antibacterial drug prophylaxis in vaccinated or unvaccinated individuals against the risks of meningococcal infection. Life-threatening and fatal meningococcal infections occurred in vaccinated and unvaccinated individuals treated with other complement inhibitors, and zilucoplan may increase susceptibility to serious meningococcal infections caused by any serogroup. Vaccination does not eliminate the risk of meningococcal infection during zilucoplan treatment, despite development of antibodies following vaccination. Closely monitor zilucoplan recipients for early signs and symptoms of meningococcal infection, and evaluate them immediately if an infection is suspected. Educate zilucoplan recipients about the signs and symptoms of infection and instruct them to seek immediate medical care if symptoms of infection occur. Meningococcal infection may become life-threatening or fatal without early recognition and treatment. Consider withholding treatment with zilucoplan in individuals undergoing treatment for serious meningococcal infection, depending on the risks of interrupting treatment. Serious infections with other Neisseria species, including disseminated gonococcal infections, have been reported in individuals treated with complement inhibitors. Administer vaccines for Streptococcus pneumoniae infection according to ACIP recommendations. Zilucoplan recipients may have increased susceptibility to other infections, including infections caused by Streptococcus pneumoniae, Haemophilus influenzae, and to a lesser extent, Neisseria gonorrhoeae. Individuals receiving zilucoplan have an increased risk of these infections, even after vaccination.

    Headline
    pregnancy
    General Information
    There are no adequate data on the developmental risks associated with zilucoplan use during human pregnancy. An increase in embryofetal death occurred at all doses tested (0, 1, 2, or 4 mg/kg/day) when zilucoplan was given subcutaneously to pregnant monkeys throughout gestation. Drug exposure (AUC) at the lowest dose tested is similar to that in humans at the maximum recommended dose of 32.4 mg/day. Placental transfer of zilucoplan to the fetal compartment occurred at a rate of 0.5% at a steady state plasma concentration of 10 mcg/mL in an ex vivo human placental transfer model, which corresponds to a therapeutic dose of 0.3 mg/kg.

Description
  Text
  Zilucoplan is an injectable complement inhibitor indicated for the treatment of generalized myasthenia gravis (gMG) in adults who are anti-acetylcholine receptor (AChR) antibody-positive. The efficacy of zilucoplan was demonstrated in a 12-week, randomized, multicenter, double-blind, placebo-controlled study in which significantly more individuals treated with zilucoplan experienced at least a 3 point improvement from baseline at treatment week 12 on the Myasthenia Gravis-Specific Activities of Daily Living scale (MG-ADL), compared treatment with placebo (73.1% vs. 46.1%; p less than 0.001). Additionally, significantly more individuals treated with zilucoplan experienced at least a 5 point improvement from baseline at treatment week 12 on the Quantitative Myasthenia Gravis (QMG) total score, compared to treatment with placebo (58% vs. 33%; p = 0.0012). Life-threatening and fatal meningococcal infections may occur during zilucoplan treatment. Vaccinate individuals for meningococcal infection (serogroups A, B, C, W, and Y) according to the Advisory Committee on Immunization Practices (ACIP) recommendations for individuals receiving a complement inhibitor at least 2 weeks prior to zilucoplan initiation. Zilucoplan is only available through a risk evaluation and mitigation program called Zilbrysq REMS due to the risk of meningococcal infection.

Mechanism Of Action
  Text
  The exact mechanism of action by which zilucoplan exerts its therapeutic effect in generalized myasthenia gravis is unknown. It is presumed to involve reduction of C5b-9 deposition at the neuromuscular junction. Zilucoplan binds to the complement protein C5 and inhibits cleavage to C5a and C5b, preventing the formation of the terminal complement complex, C5b-9.

Pharmacokinetics
  Route Specific Pharmacokinetics
    Name
    Subcutaneous Route
    Description
    Increases in the peak plasma concentration of zilucoplan were approximately dose proportional and increases in the area under the curve (AUC) was less than dose proportional following single daily subcutaneous administration of 0.05 mg/kg, 0.1 mg/kg, 0.2 mg/kg, and 0.4 mg/kg in healthy subjects. Following single and multiple daily subcutaneous injection of zilucoplan 0.3 mg/kg in healthy subjects, peak plasma concentration was reached within 3 to 6 hours post dose. Daily dosing of zilucoplan 0.3 mg/kg for 14 days resulted in approximate 3-fold increases in peak plasma concentration and exposure (AUCtau). Steady state trough concentrations of zilucoplan were reached by 4 weeks of treatment and remained consistent through 12 weeks of daily subcutaneous administration of 0.3 mg/kg. The AUC of both metabolites are approximately 10% of the parent AUC. The pharmacokinetics of zilucoplan are not time dependent.

  Text
  Zilucoplan is administered subcutaneously. The mean volume of distribution at steady state is 3.51 L. Zilucoplan and 2 major metabolites, RA103488 and RA102758, are more than 99% bound to plasma proteins. Zilucoplan is a peptide that is expected to be degraded into small peptides and amino acids via catabolic pathways. Metabolism through CYP4F2 produces the active metabolite RA103488, and the inactive metabolite RA102758 is formed by protease meditated degradation. The pharmacological activity of RA103488 is similar to zilucoplan but is present at a much lower concentration compared to zilucoplan. The mean terminal half-life of zilucoplan is approximately 172 hours (7 to 8 days). Less than 1% of a dose of zilucoplan is excreted in the urine and feces as zilucoplan and its metabolites.
 
Affected cytochrome P450 isoenzymes and drug transporters: CYP4F2
Zilucoplan is a substrate of CYP4F2. Zilucoplan is not a substrate of major CYP450 isoenzymes (CYP1A2, CYP2B6, CYP2C8, CYP2C9, CYP2C19, CYP2D6, CYP3A) or transporters (P-gp, BCRP, OATP1B1, OATP1B3). Clinically relevant interactions between zilucoplan and substrates of CYP450 isoenzymes (CYP1A2, CYP2B6, CYP2C8, CYP2C9, CYP2C19, CYP2D6, CYP3A, CYP4F), uridine diphosphoglucuronosyl transferases (UGT1A1, UGT1A3, UGT1A4, UGT1A6, UGT1A9, UGT2B7, UGT2B15), and transporters (P-gp, BCRP, OATP1B1, OATP1B3, OAT1, OAT3, MATE1, MATE2-K, OCT1, OCT2) are unlikely.

Preparation
If the syringe has been stored in the refrigerator, remove the syringe from the refrigerator and allow it to warm to room temperature on a clean, flat surface for approximately 30 to 45 minutes. Avoid placing the syringe in direct sunlight. Do not heat the syringe or place in a microwave.
Storage: If needed , syringes may be stored at room temperature up to 30 degrees C (86 degrees F) in the original carton to protect them from light for a single period of up to 3 months. After the syringe(s) have been stored at room temperature, do not place them back in the refrigerator. Throw them away if not used within the 3-month period or if the expiration date has passed, whichever occurs first. Each prefilled syringe is single-dose only; zilucoplan does not contain preservatives.
 
Subcutaneous Injection
Chose an injection site on the abdomen, thighs, or back of the upper arms. Do not inject into areas where the skin is tender, bruised, red, or hard. Avoid injection into scars or stretch marks. Injection into the upper, outer arm should be performed by a caregiver. Rotate injection sites for each administration.
Pinch up the appropriate area of skin. Insert the entire needle into the pinched skin at a 45 to 90-degree angle and hold in place. Release the pinched skin. Push the plunger all the way down as directed to inject the medicine dosage fully.
Slowly release the plunger head by lifting your thumb. After a complete injection, the needle guard will cover the needle; you may hear a "click".
Discard the syringe in an appropriate sharps container.
Missed Dose: If a dose is missed, administer the missed dose as soon as possible and then resume dosing at the next regularly scheduled time. Do not inject more than 1 dose per day.
