          Dosage Description
          Dose volume depends on lesion size assessed on each treatment day. 1 mL/cm of the largest dimension of the tumor for a maximum of 10 mL across all lesions treated with each dose. Administer intratumor injections every 2 weeks for 8 consecutive doses starting at a concentration of 10^6 plaque-forming units (PFU)/mL at week 1, followed by 10^7 PFU/mL for subsequent weeks. Prioritize the most rapidly growing and largest new or existing lesions. If no confirmed disease progression, may retreat at a concentration of 10^7 PFU/mL every 2 weeks for up to 16 additional doses. Administer in combination with nivolumab IV starting at week 3 for up to a total of 24 months. An open-label, single-arm phase 1/2 study (IGNYTE; n = 140) evaluated stage IIIB, IIIC, or IV unresectable advanced melanoma patients who had previously been treated with at least 8 consecutive weeks of immediate prior anti-PD-1-based therapy and experienced disease progression. The objective response rate was 24.2% and the median duration of response (DOR) was 14.1 months in patients treated with vusolimogene oderparepvec plus nivolumab. DOR of 6 months or greater occurred in 86.1% of patients and DOR of 12 months or greater in 54.6% of patients. In this study, nivolumab (240 mg IV every 2 weeks) was administered starting with the second dose of vusolimogene oderparepvec for 16 weeks, followed by single-agent nivolumab (480 mg IV every 4 weeks) for up to a total of 24 months. At investigator discretion following the initial 8 doses, up to 16 additional doses of vusolimogene oderparepvec (10^7 PFU/mL every 2 weeks) could be given either in combination with nivolumab or as monotherapy if nivolumab was previously stopped due to toxicity.

Description
  Text
  Vusolimogene oderparepvec-wtpg is a genetically modified oncolytic viral therapy, indicated in combination with nivolumab for adults with unresectable advanced cutaneous melanoma who experienced disease progression with a programmed death receptor-1 (PD-1)-blocking antibody-based regimen. It is for intratumor injection only. There is a potential risk for herpetic infection or reactivation after administration. Immune-mediated adverse reactions including colitis, hepatitis, myocarditis, neuropathy, capillary leak syndrome, dermatitis, and vitiligo have been reported with vusolimogene oderparepvec and nivolumab therapy. Vusolimogene oderparepvec was approved by the FDA in August 2026.

Mechanism Of Action
  Text
  Vusolimogene oderparepvec is an oncolytic immunotherapy engineered from a new clinical strain of herpes simplex virus type 1 (HSV-1) selected for its enhanced oncolytic activity. It preferentially replicates within tumor cells due to the deletion of HSV-1 neurovirulence factors ICP34.5 and ICP47. Once incorporated into tumor cells, the virus replicates leading to tumor lysis, infiltration of T cells, and release of proinflammatory molecules, and tumor and viral antigens. Vusolimogene oderparepvec also expresses granulocyte-macrophage colony stimulating factor (GM-CSF) and the fuosgenic gibbon ape leukemia virus glycoprotein with the R sequence deleted (GALV-GP-R-) to enhance direct oncolytic activity, promote immunogenic cell death, and increase overall systemic immune activation. Specifically, GALV-GP-R- increases direct tumor killing while GM-CSF activates and matures dendritic cells and monocytes. In preclinical models, immune cells exposed to vusolimogene oderparepvec activated STING-dependent interferon signaling resulting in activation of JAK-STAT signaling and increased expression of MHC class I and PD-L1. The induction of PD-L1 expression by vusolimogene oderparepvec is the mechanistic rationale for combination with PD-1 blockade.

Vusolimogene oderparepvec DNA was detected in blood samples from 19% (53 of 274) of patients within 6 hours of injection with decreasing levels thereafter; it was detected in 0.2% (3 of 1,976) of urine samples. Vusolimogene oderparepvec DNA was also detected on the surface of injected lesions in 42% of patients (112 of 266), and 18% (358 of 1,947) of samples and remained present at the injection site up to 15 days following injection in approximately 20% of patients. Samples from the exterior of injection site dressings tested positive for vusolimogene oderparepvec DNA in 21% (43 of 207) of patients; positive samples were further assessed for live vusolimogene oderparepvec. Infectious vusolimogene oderparepvec was detected in 4 out of 314 samples; however, no transmission of vusolimogene oderparepvec to close contacts was reported. No differences in the incidence of detectable vusolimogene oderparepvec DNA were observed between patients who were HSV-1 seronegative or seropositive at baseline. Vusolimogene oderparepvec is cleared from the blood and urine after 30 days with no detectable vusolimogene oderparepvec DNA in blood, urine, injection site(s), injection-site(s) dressing, or oral mucosa at 30 days after the end of therapy.

Administration
  General Information
  For storage information, see the specific product information within the How Supplied section.
CAUTION: Observe and exercise universal precautions for handling, preparing, administering, and disposing of biohazardous drugs.
Healthcare providers, caregivers, close contacts, pregnant individuals, newborns, and patients should avoid direct contact with injected tumors, dressings, or bodily fluids of patients as accidental exposure of vusolimogene oderparepvec to close contacts may lead to transmission of herpetic infection.
Wear protective gloves when applying or changing dressings and dispose of used vials and all materials that may have come into contact with vusolimogene oderparepvec (including used dressings, gloves, and cleaning materials) as biohazardous waste.
For spills or surfaces that have come into contact with vusolimogene oderparepvec, clean the area using a broad spectrum virucidal agent such as 70% isopropanol alcohol or 1% sodium hypochlorite.
Patients should place used dressings, gloves and cleaning materials into a sealed plastic bag and dispose in household waste.

Preparation:
Determine the total volume needed.
Keeping in the original container, thaw vusolimogene oderparepvec vials at room temperature (15 to 25 degrees C or 59 to 77 degrees F) or refrigerated (2 to 8 degrees C or 36 to 46 degrees F).
Swirl gently; do not shake. Inspect vial(s); particles may be visible. If settlement occurs, re-suspend by gently swirling.
Storage after thawing: The 10^6 PFU/mL vial is stable for up to 2 days when refrigerated (2 to 8 degrees C or 36 to 46 degrees F) or up to 24 hours at room temperature (15 to 25 degrees C or 59 to 77 degrees F); the 10^7 PFU/mL vial is stable for up to 10 days when refrigerated (2 to 8 degrees C or 36 to 46 degrees F) or up to 24 hours at room temperature (15 to 25 degrees C or 59 to 77 degrees F). Protect from light in the original vial and carton. Do not refreeze.
Withdraw the required dose volume from the vial(s) into the syringe(s) for administration.
Select needles between 17 gauge and 27 gauge based on the location and characteristics of the tumors to be injected; a higher gauge needle allows for smaller puncture site and reduces leakage of vusolimogene oderparepvec along the needle tract. A lower needle gauge is needed for fibrotic or deeper lesions.
Administration:
Administer vusolimogene oderparepvec within 2 hours of syringe preparation.
Do not inject vusolimogene oderparepvec into brain or spinal cord tumors or scalp tumors with moderate to extensive bone erosion. Avoid tumors that encase, invade, or occlude major blood vessels, major nerve structures, or airways. Avoid direct injection into blood vessels.
Clean the lesion and surrounding areas with alcohol; treat the periphery of the lesion with a topical or local anesthetic if necessary. Do not inject anesthetic agent directly into lesion.
Intratumor injection into Nonulcerated Superficial Tumors:
Administer vusolimogene oderparepvec by direct intratumor injection into superficial tumors using visualization and palpation for guidance; refer to product labeling for visual depictions of injection techniques.
Avoiding areas of necrosis and targeting viable tumor tissue, insert the needle from the periphery at a shallow angle (less than 45-degree) to the base of the tumor.
Using a single insertion point, partially inject vusolimogene oderparepvec slowly.
While keeping needle inserted at a single insertion point, partially withdraw, and move the needle to different viable areas of the tumor, dispersing vusolimogene oderparepvec as uniformly as possible throughout the tumor. Continue until the full dose is dispensed.
Multiple insertion sites may be needed, depending on the tumor size.
Withdraw needle slowly from the tumor to avoid leakage at the insertion point.
Intratumor injection into Ulcerated Superficial Tumors:
Administer vusolimogene oderparepvec by direct intratumor injection into superficial tumors using visualization and palpation for guidance; refer to product labeling for visual depictions of injection techniques.
Do not inject vusolimogene oderparepvec through areas of ulceration and/or necrosis.
Insert the needle from the periphery at a shallow angle (less than 45-degree) to the base of the tumor.
Using multiple insertion points (radial insertion), partially inject vusolimogene oderparepvec, slowly dispersing it as uniformly as possible throughout the tumor. Continue until the full dose is dispersed.
Withdraw needle slowly from the tumor to avoid leakage at the insertion point.
Intratumor injection into Deep or Visceral Tumors:
Administer vusolimogene oderparepvec by direct intratumor injection into deep or visceral tumors guided by imaging technology, avoiding major vessels.
Ensure selected hepatic tumors are a safe distance from the main portal vein and common bile duct.
Ensure selected lung tumors are a safe distance from any lobar or segmental pulmonary arteries or veins or lobar or segmental bronchi. Do not inject tumors in both the right and left lung lobes on the same day.
Vusolimogene oderparepvec may be administered in deep or visceral tumors by radial injection or coaxial injection; refer to product labeling for visual depictions of injection techniques.
For administration by radial injection, insert the needle into the tumor through a single insertion point to the distal end of the tumor; multi-prong or multi-orifice needles may be used.
Partially inject vusolimogene oderparepvec slowly while withdrawing the needle from the tumor; redirect the needle into the tumor and repeat steps until the full dose is dispersed. Withdraw needle slowly from the tumor to avoid leakage at the insertion point.
For administration by coaxial injection, insert an introducer needle, if necessary, into the visceral organ where tumor is to be treated.
Pass the primed vusolimogene oderparepvec needle through the introducer needle into the target tumor and inject slowly, redirecting the needles and repeating until the full dose is dispersed. Withdraw needle slowly from the tumor to avoid leakage at the insertion point.
Post-Injection:
Change gloves and apply a sterile occlusive dressing at each injection site.
Keep injection site(s) covered for at least 48 hours; replace the dressing if it falls off.
Clean surfaces that may have come into contact with vusolimogene oderparepvec with a broad spectrum virucidal agent such as 70% isopropanol alcohol or 1% sodium hypochlorite.
Dispose of all materials (e.g. syringe, gloves, cleaning materials) as biohazardous waste.
Monitor patients undergoing injections for liver, lung or kidney tumors for procedure related reactions for at least 2 to 4 hours post injection.
