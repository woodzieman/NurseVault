Description
  Text
  Bismuth subgallate is an oral agent that is taken for the purpose of eliminating or reducing odor from flatulence (gas) and stool (feces). Products for non-prescription (OTC) use are approved for use in adults and children 12 years of age and older. It is not intended to reduce odor that is due to poor personal hygiene. Oral bismuth subgallate has been used as an FDA-approved OTC drug product since at least 1974.

Mechanism Of Action
  Text
  Bismuth subgallate may decrease the odor of flatulence and stool by inhibiting bacterial fermentation in the gastrointestinal tract, though the exact mechanism is unknown. Bismuth compounds appear to have antisecretory, anti-inflammatory, and antibacterial properties, which may help with reducing odor from flatulence and stool.

Pharmacokinetics
  Text
  Bismuth subgallate is administered orally. In the stomach, bismuth compounds are converted to insoluble salts (including oxide, hydroxide, oxychloride, and citrate) that are poorly absorbed. Less than 1% of bismuth subgallate is absorbed systemically. Unabsorbable bismuth is excreted in the feces; any bismuth that is absorbed is excreted in the urine. The plasma half-life is approximately 5 days.
 
Affected cytochrome P450 isoenzymes and drug transporters: None
