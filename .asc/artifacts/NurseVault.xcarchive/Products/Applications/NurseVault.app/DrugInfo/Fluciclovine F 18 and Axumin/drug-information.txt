Description
  Text
  Fluciclovine F 18 is an intravenous, radioactive diagnostic agent for use with positron emission tomography (PET) to evaluate men with suspected prostate cancer recurrence. Following intravenous administration, fluciclovine F 18 is actively transported into prostate cancer cells, where peak accumulation occurs 4 to 10 minutes after the injection. Localization of prostate cancer recurrence in sites typical for prostate cancer recurrence is based on fluciclovine F 18 uptake in comparison with normal tissue background. However, because fluciclovine F 18 is not specific for prostate cancer, a positive or negative image does not confirm or rule out the presence of recurrent prostate cancer. An area identified by the PET scan as being a suspected recurrence site should be histopathologically evaluated for confirmation. Fluciclovine F 18 was FDA-approved in May 2016.

Mechanism Of Action
  Text
  Fluciclovine F 18 is a radiolabeled, synthetic amino acid transported across mammalian cells by amino acid transporters, such as LAT-1 and ASCT2, which are upregulated in prostate cancer cells; thus, these cancer cells accumulate more fluciclovine F 18 than the surrounding normal tissue. Localization of prostate cancer recurrence in sites typical for prostate cancer recurrence is based on fluciclovine F 18 uptake in comparison to tissue background. Areas where fluciclovine F 18 has accumulated can be identified using positron emission tomography (PET) to detect the F 18 radioisotope. For small lesions (less than 1 cm in diameter), focal uptake greater than the blood pool should be considered suspicious for prostate cancer recurrence. For larger lesions, uptake equal to or more than the bone marrow is considered suspicious for prostate cancer recurrence. The contrast between tumor and normal tissue is highest at 4 to 10 minutes after drug administration. Of note, fluciclovine F 18 is not specific for prostate cancer, as increased uptake may occur with other types of cancer and with benign prostatic hypertrophy in primary prostate cancer; histopathological evaluation of suspected recurrence site is recommended.

    Description
    The amount of radiation absorbed following exposure to the recommended fluciclovine F 18 dose is 8 mSv, with the highest magnitude of radiation going to the pancreas (38 mGy), myocardium (19 mGy), and uterine wall (17 mGy). Use of a computerized tomography (CT) scan will increase radiation exposure. The physical half-life of fluciclovine F 18 is 109.7 minutes.
  Text
  Fluciclovine F 18 is administered intravenously. Once administered, fluciclovine F 18 distributes to the liver (14% of administered activity), red bone marrow (12%), lungs (7%), myocardium (4%), pancreas (3%), and skeletal muscles. Within the first 4 hours and 24 hours following injection, 3% and 5% of the administered radioactivity were excreted in urine, respectively.

  General Information
  For storage information, see the specific product information within the How Supplied section.
 
NOTE: Patient preparation before the fluciclovine F 18 positron emission tomography (PET) scan includes instructing the patient:
to avoid significant exercise for at least 1 day before the scan
to not eat or drink for at least 4 hours before the scan (other than sips of water for taking medications)
to urinate approximately 30 to 60 minutes before the scan and to avoid further urination until after the scan has been completed
NOTE: Fluciclovine F 18 is a radioactive material. To minimize radiation exposure to occupational workers and patients, observe appropriate precautions that are consistent with proper patient management. Radiopharmaceuticals should only be used by nuclear physicians and/or radiopharmacists who are qualified by training and experienced in the safe use and handling of radioactive material, and whose experience and training have been approved by the appropriate governmental agency authorized to license the use of radiopharmaceuticals.
NOTE: Storage and disposal should be controlled in compliance with appropriate regulations of the governmental agency authorized to license the use of radiopharmaceuticals.
