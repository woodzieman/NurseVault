    Headline
    glaucoma
    General Information
    Sofpironium is contraindicated for use in patients with glaucoma because it produces mydriasis which can increase intraocular pressure.
    Headline
    Hypersensitivity
    General Information
    This medication is contraindicated in patients with a history of hypersensitivity to it or any of its components.
    Headline
    Sjogren syndrome
    General Information
    Sofpironium is contraindicated in individuals with Sjogren syndrome due to risk for exacerbation by the anticholinergic effect of sofpironium.
    Headline
    myasthenia gravis
    General Information
    Sofpironium is contraindicated in patients with myasthenia gravis because its anticholinergic effects may exacerbate the symptoms of the disease (e.g., muscle weakness or fatigue).
    Headline
    ileus, toxic megacolon, ulcerative colitis
    General Information
    The anticholinergic effects of sofpironium on the gastrointestinal tract may exacerbate some gastrointestinal disorders. For this reason, sofpironium is contraindicated in patients with paralytic ileus, severe ulcerative colitis, and toxic megacolon complicating ulcerative colitis.
    Headline
    hemorrhagic shock
    General Information
    Use of sofpironium is contraindicated in patients with unstable cardiovascular status due to hemorrhagic shock. Sofpironium, like other anticholinergic drugs, may cause tachycardia through its actions on the SA node; thereby increasing the oxygen demand on the heart.
    Headline
    bladder obstruction, prostatic hypertrophy, urinary retention
    General Information
    Use sofpironium cautiously in patients with a history or presence of documented urinary retention. Healthcare providers and patients should be alert for signs and symptoms of urinary retention (e.g., difficulty passing urine, distended bladder), especially in patients with prostatic hypertrophy, bladder-neck obstruction (bladder obstruction) or other urinary tract obstruction. Instruct patients to immediately discontinue use of sofpironium and consult a healthcare provider if any signs or symptoms of urinary retention develop.
    Headline
    conditions contributing to an elevated core body temperature
    General Information
    Heat illness (i.e., hyperpyrexia, heat stroke) due to decreased sweating may occur when sofpironium is administered in the presence of conditions contributing to an elevated core body temperature. Instruct sofpironium recipients to watch for generalized lack of sweating when in hot or very warm temperatures, and to avoid use if not sweating in these conditions.
    Headline
    activities requiring coordination and concentration, driving or operating machinery
    General Information
    Transient blurred vision may occur with use of sofpironium. Advise individuals to discontinue sofpironium and avoid engaging in activities that require clear vision, such as activities requiring coordination and concentration, driving or operating machinery, or performing hazardous work, until symptoms have resolved.
    Headline
    pregnancy
    General Information
    There are no available data on the use of sofpironium during pregnancy to assess the risk of major birth defects, miscarriage, or adverse outcomes. In animal reproduction studies, subcutaneous administration to pregnant rats and rabbits during organogenesis showed no significant adverse effects at doses of 31 and 10 times the maximum recommended human dose, respectively.
    Headline
    breast-feeding
    General Information
    There are no data on the presence of sofpironium in human milk, its effects on the breast-fed child, or its effect on milk production. Sofpironium is present in animal milk. Consider the benefits of breast-feeding, the patient's clinical need for treatment, and any potential adverse effects on the breast-fed child from the medication or from the patient's underlying medical condition.

Description
  Text
  Sofpironium is an anticholinergic indicated for the treatment of primary axillary hyperhidrosis in adults and pediatric patients 9 years of age and older. It is available as a topical gel that is applied once daily at bedtime. Avoid using in those with medical conditions that may be exacerbated by the anticholinergic actions of sofpironium. Persons using sofpironium should watch for a generalized lack of sweating when in hot or very warm environments and avoid use if not sweating in these conditions. Sofpironium was approved in June 2024.

Mechanism Of Action
  Text
  Sofpironium bromide is a competitive inhibitor of acetylcholine receptors that are located on certain peripheral tissues, including sweat glands. Sofpironium bromide indirectly reduces the rate of sweating by preventing the stimulation of these receptors.

    Pediatrics
    Description
    The pharmacokinetics of sofpironium in pediatric patients (aged 9 to 16 years) were similar to those observed in adults. Following topical application to the axillae, a maximum plasma concentration of 1.3 ng/mL was achieved within 4 hours, and the mean AUC of 14.6 ng x hour/mL on day 1. After 24 weeks of dosing, trough concentrations of sofpironium were low and there was no evidence of drug accumulation.
  Text
  Sofpironium is applied topically. The plasma protein binding of sofpironium is around 34.8% to 37.8%. The major sofpironium metabolite (BBI-4010) had a plasma protein binding around 2.3% to 3.7%. Sofpironium is metabolized by nonenzymatic hydrolysis, CYP2D6 and CYP3A4 mediated-oxidative metabolism, and glycine conjugation. Urinary excretion of sofpironium and its metabolite were less than 0.5% of the applied dose.
 
Affected Cytochrome P450 (CYP450) isoenzymes and drug transporters: CYP3A4, OCT2, MATE1, or MATE2-K
 
In vitro studies
Sofpironium is an inhibitor of CYP2D6, CYP3A4, OCT1, OCT2, and MATE1 in vitro. Sofpironium is not an inducer of CYP1A2, CYP2B6, or CYP3A4.
