    Headline
    pregnancy
    General Information
    Animal development and reproduction studies have not been conducted with riboflavin 5'-phosphate ophthalmic solution. Since it is not known whether the epithelium-on corneal collagen cross-linking procedure can cause fetal harm or affect reproduction capacity, it should not be performed during pregnancy.
    Headline

    General Information
    Use riboflavin 5'-phosphate ophthalmic solution with caution during breast-feeding. There are no data on the presence of riboflavin 5'-phosphate ophthalmic solution in human milk, their effects on the breastfed child, or their effects on milk production. The developmental and health benefits of breast-feeding should be considered along with the clinical need for the procedure and any potential adverse effects on the breastfed child from the procedure or the underlying maternal condition.

Description
  Text
  Riboflavin 5'-phosphate (Vitamin B2) ophthalmic solutions are topical ophthalmic photoenhancers used as part of corneal collagen cross-linking procedures in individuals with corneal ectatic disease. Epioxa HD and Epioxa are indicated, with the O2n System and Boost Goggles, for epithelium-on corneal collagen cross-linking for treatment of keratoconus, whereas Photrexa Viscous and Photrexa are indicated, with the KXL System, for corneal collagen cross-linking in progressive keratoconus and corneal ectasia following refractive surgery. Riboflavin 5'-phosphate acts as a photoenhancer that generates singlet oxygen under ultraviolet-A treatment conditions to facilitate corneal collagen cross-linking. Riboflavin 5'-phosphate was initially FDA-approved in 2016.

Mechanism Of Action
  Text
  Riboflavin 5'-phosphate sodium (vitamin B2) is the precursor of two coenzymes, flavin adenine dinucleotide and flavin mononucleotide, which catalyze oxidation-reduction reactions in multiple metabolic pathways. Under the conditions used for corneal collagen cross-linking, riboflavin 5'-phosphate functions as a photoenhancer and generates singlet oxygen, which is responsible for the cross-linking.

Administration
  General Information
  For storage information, see the specific product information within the How Supplied section.
  Route Specific Administrations
    Name
    Ophthalmic Administration
    Description
    Epioxa HD/Epioxa
Apply topical anesthetic and insert a lid speculum.
Using a cellulose spear sponge soaked with Epioxa HD, remove the mucin layer from the corneal surface without debriding the corneal epithelium by swiping the sponge 4 to 10 times horizontally and vertically.
Instill 2 drops of Epioxa HD every 60 seconds for 4 minutes, then 2 drops of Epioxa every 30 seconds for 6 minutes.
Gently rinse the corneal surface with approximately 5 mL of balanced salt solution. Perform ultrasound pachymetry; do not irradiate if corneal thickness is less than 325 microns.
Apply the Boost Goggles and start oxygen flow per the device instructions. Center the O2n optical head and irradiate for 11 minutes 6 seconds at 30 mW/cm2 with a 1 second on/1 second off cycle at 365 nm. Instill balanced salt solution every 2 minutes, or more frequently if needed, to maintain hydration during irradiation.
When irradiation stops, discontinue oxygen flow, remove the Boost Goggles and lid speculum, and apply a bandage contact lens.
Storage: Store refrigerated at 2 to 8 degrees C. Do not freeze. Minimize exposure of syringes to light after removal from protective packaging. Discard syringes after use.
Photrexa Viscous/Photrexa
Using topical anesthesia, debride the epithelium to a diameter of approximately 9 mm using standard aseptic technique.
Instill 1 drop of Photrexa Viscous every 2 minutes for 30 minutes. At the end of the soak, examine the eye under slit lamp for a yellow flare in the anterior chamber. If flare is absent, instill 1 additional drop every 2 minutes for 2 to 3 more drops and recheck; repeat as needed.
Once flare is observed, perform ultrasound pachymetry. If corneal thickness is less than 400 microns, instill 2 drops of Photrexa every 5 to 10 seconds until corneal thickness increases to at least 400 microns. Do not irradiate unless both the 400 micron threshold and yellow flare are present.
Irradiate the eye for 30 continuous minutes at 3 mW/cm2 and 365 nm with the KXL System. During irradiation, continue Photrexa Viscous instillation every 2 minutes for the 30-minute irradiation period.
Discard syringe(s) after use.
Storage: Supplied as a 2-syringe kit containing 1 syringe of Photrexa Viscous and 1 syringe of Photrexa. Store at 2 to 8 degrees C. Minimize syringe light exposure once removed from protective packaging. Discard syringe after use.
