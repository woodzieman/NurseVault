    Headline
    pregnancy
    General Information
    Discontinue lerodalcibep when pregnancy is recognized unless the benefits of therapy outweigh the potential risks to the fetus. There are insufficient data on the use of lerodalcibep during pregnancy to evaluate for a drug associated risk of major birth defects, miscarriage, or adverse outcomes for the pregnant individual or fetus. Due to the lack of human data, guidelines recommend avoiding the use of other PCSK9 inhibitors during pregnancy. FDA-approved labeling advises considering the individual's ongoing need for therapy. Lerodalcibep may cause fetal harm based upon its mechanism of action. Lerodalcibep increases LDL-C uptake and lowers LDL-C concentrations in the circulation, therefore decreasing cholesterol and potentially other biologically active substances derived from cholesterol. Cholesterol and other products of the cholesterol biosynthesis pathway are essential components for fetal development, including synthesis of steroids and cell membranes. In animal reproduction studies involving cynomolgus monkeys, no adverse embryo-fetal or developmental effects (up to 6 months of age) were observed with lerodalcibep administration during organogenesis through parturition at subcutaneous doses of 30 and 100 mg/kg/week (exposures up to 119 times the maximum recommended human dose (MRHD) based on 300 mg every month). An assessment of immune function in the infant monkeys showed no treatment-related adverse effects; however, infant monkeys had measurable lerodalcibep serum concentrations of approximately 2% to 7% of maternal serum concentrations on postnatal days 14, 21, and 28. These findings indicate that lerodalcibep may be transmitted from the mother to the developing fetus. Consider the benefits and risks of lerodalcibep when prescribing during pregnancy.
    Headline
    breast-feeding
    General Information
    Use lerodalcibep with caution during breast-feeding. There is no information regarding the presence of lerodalcibep in human milk, the effects on the breastfed infant, or the effects on milk production. Following subcutaneous administration of lerodalcibep 30 and 100 mg/kg/week (exposures up to 119-fold the maximum recommended human dose (MRHD) by AUC) during organogenesis through parturition, low concentrations of lerodalcibep were present in the milk of lactating monkeys collected on post-partum day 7 and 14. Description
  Text
  Lerodalcibep is a proprotein convertase subtilisin/kexin type 9 (PCSK9) inhibitor indicated as an adjunct to diet and exercise for the treatment of hypercholesterolemia, including heterozygous familial hypercholesterolemia (HeFH). Lerodalcibep binds to and inhibits PCSK9 from binding to low-density lipoprotein receptors (LDLR) on the hepatocyte surface, which increases the number of LDLRs available to clear LDL leading to a decrease in LDL-C concentrations. In clinical trials, lerodalcibep produced mean placebo-adjusted reductions from baseline in LDL-C of 50% to 59%. Additionally, the majority of lerodalcibep-treated subjects achieved both a reduction in LDL-C of 50% or greater and recommended LDL-C targets. Lerodalcibep was FDA-approved in 2025.

Mechanism Of Action
  Text
  Lerodalcibep is a recombinant fusion protein that binds to and inhibits proprotein convertase subtilisin/kexin type 9 (PCSK9) binding to low-density lipoprotein receptors (LDLR). PCSK9 binds to LDLR on the hepatocyte surface to promote LDLR degradation within the liver. LDLR is the primary receptor that clears circulating LDL; with lerodalcibep inhibiting the binding of PCSK9 to LDLR, the number of LDLRs available to clear LDL increases and LDL-C concentrations decrease.
 
Pharmacodynamics
After subcutaneous administration of 300 mg lerodalcibep every 4 weeks, more than 90% suppression of PCSK9 occurs within 24 hours of dosing and is maintained throughout the dosing interval, returning toward baseline during the last 7 to 10 days of the monthly dosing period.

    Description
    The median estimated subcutaneous bioavailability of lerodalcibep is 83%. Following a subcutaneous dose of 300 mg lerodalcibep once every four weeks, steady state is reached after 2 to 3 days and the mean Cmax is 31.4 mcg/mL and AUC is 12,600 hours x mcg/mL. At the recommended approved dose, lerodalcibep accumulation is approximately 30%. The median Tmax is 6 days at steady state.

  Lerodalcibep is administered subcutaneously. Lerodalcibep has a Vd of 5.3L and does not extensively distribute into tissues. Since lerodalcibep is a protein, it is expected to degrade to small peptides and amino acids. Clearance of free lerodalcibep is 0.36 L/day (30%) with an estimated half-life of approximately 10 days.       Description
      If stored in the refrigerator, allow the prefilled syringe or autoinjector to warm to room temperature up to 25 degrees C (77 degrees F) for at least 30 minutes prior to administration. Keep the prefilled syringe or autoinjector in the carton during this warm-up period to protect from light. Allow the solution to warm naturally. Do not shake.
Select an injection site on the abdomen (except for the 2-inch area right around the navel), front of the upper thighs, or, if administered by a caregiver or healthcare provider, the back of upper arms. Clean the skin of the injection site with an alcohol wipe.
Do not inject into an area of skin that is tender, red, bruised, or indurated. Avoid areas with scars or stretch marks. Do not administer through clothing.
Rotate the injection site with each injection.
Do not administer with other injectable drugs at the same injection site.
Single-Dose Prefilled Syringe Administration
Remove the plastic tray from the carton. Carefully remove the prefilled syringe from the plastic tray by peeling off the paper seal and gently pressing the middle of the tray's back to release the prefilled syringe. Do not pick up or pull the prefilled syringe by the plunger rod or gray needle cap as this could damage the syringe.
Remove the gray needle cap from the prefilled syringe by pulling it straight off. Do not twist the needle cap. Do not touch the plunger rod as this may cause the prefilled syringe to release medicine prior to injection. There may be a drop of liquid at the tip of the needle or air bubbles present in the solution; this is normal. Do not remove air bubbles if present.
Gently pinch a fold of skin at the injection site with one hand and hold the pinch throughout the injection. With the other hand, insert the needle with a quick dart-like motion into the skin at a 45 to 90-degree angle.
Slowly push the plunger rod all the way down until the prefilled syringe is empty. Once empty, remove the prefilled syringe from the skin.
Dispose of the used prefilled syringe in an appropriate sharps disposal container immediately following the injection. Do not re-cap or reuse the syringe.
Storage: Store refrigerated at 2 to 8 degrees C (36 to 46 degrees F) in the original carton to protect from light. Do not freeze. If needed, lerodalcibep may be stored in the original carton at room temperature of 68 to 77 degrees F (20 to 25 degrees C) for up to 3 months after being removed from the refrigerator. If not used within 3 months, discard lerodalcibep.
Single-Dose Autoinjector Administration
Do not remove the clear protective cap until ready to inject. Air bubbles may be present. Do not try to remove the bubbles.
Hold the autoinjector in a horizontal position and pull the clear protective cap off to see the purple needle shield. Do not touch the purple needle shield or the needle inside.
Do not put the clear protective cap back on the autoinjector.
Hold the autoinjector at a 90-degree angle and press it firmly against the cleaned injection site. Make sure the viewing window is visible.
Press the autoinjector downward against the skin. Two audible clicks should be heard.
The first click means the injection has started. The second click means the injection is almost done.
Do not lift the autoinjector from the skin during injection.
After the second click, hold the autoinjector in place for 10 seconds.
Check that the green plunger has stopped moving before removing the autoinjector from the injection site.
Remove the autoinjector from the injection site while keeping it at the same angle. Do not tilt the autoinjector.
Dispose of the used autoinjector in an appropriate sharps disposal container immediately following the injection. Do not reuse the autoinjector.
Storage: Store refrigerated at 2 to 8 degrees C (36 to 46 degrees F) in the original carton to protect from light. Do not freeze. If needed, lerodalcibep may be stored in the original carton at room temperature up to 77 degrees F (25 degrees C) for up to 3 months after being removed from the refrigerator. Do not place the autoinjector back into the refrigerator after it has reached room temperature. If not used within 3 months, discard lerodalcibep.

Dosage Adjustment Guidelines
  Hepatic Impairment
  No dosage adjustment is needed for mild (total bilirubin 1 to 1.5 times the upper limit of normal (ULN) or aspartate aminotransaminase (AST) more than the ULN) or moderate (total bilirubin 1.5 to 3 times the ULN) hepatic impairment. Lerodalcibep-liga has not been studied in individuals with severe hepatic impairment.
  Renal Impairment
  No dosage adjustment is needed for mild or moderate renal impairment (eGFR 30 to less than 90 mL/minute). Lerodalcibep-liga has not been studied in individuals with severe renal impairment (eGFR less than 30 mL/minute).
