    Headline
    pregnancy
    General Information
    There are insufficient data on the use of plozasiran during human pregnancy to inform a drug-associated risk of major birth defects, miscarriage, or adverse maternal or fetal outcomes. Triglyceride concentrations increase during the third trimester of pregnancy, putting people with underlying defects in lipid metabolism, such as familial chylomicronemia syndrome, at risk of severe gestational hypertriglyceridemia and increasing the risk of acute pancreatitis during pregnancy. In animal reproduction studies of subcutaneous administration of plozasiran during organogenesis up to 23 and 140 times the maximum recommended human dose (MRHD) in pregnant rats or rabbits, respectively, no adverse plozasiran-related development effects were observed. In an embryo-fetal development study in pregnant rats, maternally toxic doses were associated with an increase in post-implantation loss and mean number of late resorptions at 60 mg/kg (23 times the MRHD) and early deliveries, reduced fetal body weight, and fetal skeletal developmental variations at 15 mg/kg or higher (6 times the MRHD). In a pre- and postnatal development study in rats receiving plozasiran at 0, 8, 24, or 80 mg/kg by subcutaneous injection once a week from gestation day 6 through lactation day 17, plozasiran increased the number of stillborn offspring, which results in a reduction in the in live birth index at 80 mg/kg (31 times the MRHD based on BSA). Decreases in offspring body weight and offspring survival were seen at plozasiran doses of 24 mg/kg or more (9 times the MRHD based on BSA). No adverse effects were noted on offspring development up to 80 mg/kg (31 times the MRHD based on BSA).
    Headline
    breast-feeding
    General Information
    Use plozasiran with caution during breast-feeding. There are no data on the presence of plozasiran in human milk, its effects on the breast-fed child, or its effects on milk production. Oligonucleotide-based products typically have poor oral bioavailability; thus, if plozasiran is in breast milk, it is unlikely to result in clinically relevant concentrations in the breast-fed child. Consider the benefits of breast-feeding, the individual's clinical need for treatment, and any potential adverse effects on the breast-fed child from the medication or from the individual's underlying medical condition.

    Hypercholesterolemia, specifically increases in low-density lipoprotein cholesterol (LDL-C) and increases in total apolipoprotein B (apoB), have occurred with plozasiran use. In a clinical trial of individuals with familial chylomicronemia syndrome (FCS), plozasiran-treated individuals had a mean 112% increase in LDL-C and mean 27% increase in total apoB compared to a mean increase of 20% and 12% for both LDL-C and apoB in placebo-treated subjects, respectively. Description
  Text
  Plozasiran is an apolipoprotein C-III (apoC-III)-directed small interfering ribonucleic acid (siRNA) indicated as an adjunct to diet to reduce triglycerides in adults with familial chylomicronemia syndrome (FCS); it is designated as an orphan drug by the FDA for this indication. The most common adverse effects seen in plozasiran clinical trials were hyperglycemia, headache, nausea, and injection site reactions. Plozasiran was FDA-approved in November 2025.

Mechanism Of Action
  Text
  Plozasiran is a small interfering RNA (siRNA) conjugated to a ligand containing three N-acetylgalactosamine (GaINAc) residues to enable delivery to hepatocytes. Plozasiran degrades apoC-III mRNA through the RNA interference mechanism, resulting in reduced levels of hepatic and serum apoC-III protein. Reduction of apoC-III protein leads to increased clearance of serum triglycerides. ApoC-III synthesis primarily occurs in the liver and secondarily, to a lower extent, in the intestines. Once in the plasma, apoC-III is transported by triglyceride-rich lipoproteins (TRL) and high density lipoproteins (HDL). ApoC-III inhibits lipoprotein lipase (LPL) activity and reduces the uptake of TRL remnants by the liver, which is associated with hypertriglyceridemia.
 
Pharmacodynamics:
Following subcutaneous administration of plozasiran 25 mg every 3 months, the placebo-corrected median percent reduction from baseline in fasting apoC-III was 90% at 1 month, 93% at 3 months, 82% at 6 months, 91% at 10 months, and 87% at 12 months.

  Text
  Plozasiran is administered subcutaneously. Plozasiran is 78% protein bound and has an apparent volume of distribution of approximately 146 L. Prior to uptake by hepatocytes, plozasiran distributes into plasma and extracellular body water. Plozasiran primarily undergoes metabolism by nucleases to shorter oligonucleotides of varying lengths. Approximately 16% to 19% of plozasiran dose is excreted in the urine. The mean apparent systemic clearance is 33.8 L/hour and the approximate terminal elimination half-life is 3 to 4 hours.
 
Affected cytochrome P450 isoenzymes and drug transporters: None

  Route Specific Administrations
    Administration Details
      Name
      Subcutaneous Administration
      Description
      If stored in the refrigerator, allow the solution to reach room temperature by removing it from the refrigerator 30 minutes before the injection. Do not warm by using a heat source, such as a microwave or hot water.
Do not remove the gray needle cap until you are ready to inject.
Select an injection site on the abdomen (except for a 2 inch (5 cm) area around the belly button) or front of the thigh. The outer area of the upper arm may be used as an administration site if a caregiver or care team member administers the injection.
Wash hands thoroughly with soap and water. Clean the injection site with an alcohol wipe. Allow the wiped skin to dry before injecting; do not touch the cleansed skin.
Hold the prefilled syringe with the needle facing away from you. Remove the gray needle cap by pulling it straight upwards. Avoid pushing the plunger head until it is time to inject.
Avoid injection into skin that is bruised, tender, red, hard, cut, or damaged or into areas with scars or stretch marks.
Gently pinch and hold a fold of skin at the injection site with one hand.
Hold the prefilled syringe with the other hand and insert the needle into the skin fold at a 45- to 90-degree angle. Keep the skin pinched while inserting the needle and during the injection
Push the plunger head all the way down using slow and constant pressure.
After the plunger head is pushed all the way down, gently lift the syringe up and off the skin until the needle is fully removed. Do not rub the injection site.
Dispose the used cap and syringe in a sharps disposal container. Do not put the needle cap back into the syringe.
There may be a small amount of blood or liquid at the injection site. If needed, press a cotton ball or gauze pad on the area and apply an adhesive bandage.
Storage: Store in the refrigerator at 2 to 8 degrees C (36 to 46 degrees F) in the original carton. It may also be stored at room temperature of 20 to 25 degrees C (68 to 77 degrees F) in the original carton for up to 30 days. If not used within 30 days, discard plozasiran. Do not use plozasiran if it reaches temperatures above 25 degrees C (77 degrees F).
    Name
    Injectable Administration
    Description
    Visually inspect parenteral products for particulate matter and discoloration prior to administration whenever solution and container permit. Plozasiran is a clear and colorless to yellow solution. Do not use if the solution is cloudy, discolored, or contains particulate matter.
Do not use the single-dose pre-filled syringe if the carton or syringe appears damaged, the tamper evident seal on the carton is broken, or the gray cap is not missing or not securely attached.
Missed dose: Administer the missed dose as soon as possible. Resume dosing at 3 months intervals from the date of the most recently administered dose.

Dosage Adjustment Guidelines
  Hepatic Impairment
  No dosage adjustment is needed in mild hepatic impairment (total bilirubin of 1 times the upper limit of normal [ULN] or less and AST greater than 1 times the ULN, or total bilirubin greater than 1 to 1.5 times the ULN and any AST). The impact of moderate or severe hepatic impairment on plozasiran use is unknown.
  Renal Impairment
  No dosage adjustment is needed in mild or moderate renal impairment (eGFR 30 to 90 mL/minute). The impact of severe renal impairment or end stage renal disease on plozasiran use is unknown.
