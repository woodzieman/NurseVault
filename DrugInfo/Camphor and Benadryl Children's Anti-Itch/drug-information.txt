Description
  Text
  Camphor is a topical counterirritant. It is used to treat cough due to colds and also for several dermatological conditions, such as pain, pruritus, and other skin irritation. Camphor-containing nonprescription products have been marketed since the 1980s and the drug is available as a single agent or in combination with other analgesics or anesthetics.
