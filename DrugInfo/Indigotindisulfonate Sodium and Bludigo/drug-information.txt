    cardiac conduction disorder, hypertension
    General Information
    Individuals with hypertension, heart rate or cardiac conduction disorder, or receiving concomitant medications that cause bradycardia may be at risk for elevated blood pressure, hypotension, or bradycardia after administration of indigotindisulfonate sodium. Closely monitor blood pressure and cardiac rhythm during and after indigotindisulfonate sodium administration.
    Headline
    pregnancy
    General Information
    Available data with indigotindisulfonate sodium use in human pregnancy over several decades have not identified a drug associated risk of adverse maternal and fetal adverse effects. Use of indigotindisulfonate sodium during the first trimester is rare; therefore, data are insufficient to evaluate for a drug associated risk of major birth defects and miscarriage. Animal reproduction studies using the intravenous route of administration have not been conducted. Oral administration of indigotindisulfonate sodium to pregnant rats and rabbits produced no evidence of fetal harm. However, oral bioavailability of indigotindisulfonate sodium is low (3%) and the risk of intravenous administration during pregnancy cannot be evaluated from available animal data.

Adverse Reactions
  Headline
  Adverse Reactions
  Adverse Reactions
    Headline
    asystole, AV block, bradycardia, cardiac arrest, hypertension, hypotension, sinus tachycardia
    General Information
    Severe or life-threatening cardiovascular adverse reactions have been reported after administration of indigotindisulfonate sodium. Cardiovascular adverse reactions such as asystole, cardiac arrest, cardiac arrhythmias, second degree atrioventricular block (AV block), hypotension, hypertension, bradycardia, and sinus tachycardia have been reported with indigotindisulfonate sodium in postmarketing surveillance. These reactions have been reported generally within 60 minutes after indigotindisulfonate sodium administration and required urgent intervention. Indigotindisulfonate sodium may cause vasoconstriction by interference with vasodilation caused by nitric oxide dependent mechanisms and by direct vasoconstriction. Closely monitor blood pressure and cardiac rhythm during and after indigotindisulfonate sodium administration. Interrupt administration of indigotindisulfonate sodium if cardiovascular adverse reactions are observed.

Description
  Text
  Indigotindisulfonate sodium is an intravenous diagnostic dye indicated for use as a visualization aid in the cystoscopic assessment of the integrity of the ureters in adults after urological or gynecological open, robotic, or endoscopic surgical procedures. Severe or life-threatening cardiovascular reactions and serious anaphylactic reactions have been reported after indigotindisulfonate sodium administration, requiring urgent intervention. Indigotindisulfonate sodium has been reported to interfere with light absorption and transiently interfere with pulse oximetric methods, resulting in the potential for artifactual reduction in oxygen saturation (SpO2) with administration of indigotindisulfonate sodium to anesthetized patients.

Mechanism Of Action
  Text
  Indigotindisulfonate sodium is a dye excreted by the kidney through tubular secretion and enhances visualization of the ureteral orifices by its deep blue color. Detection of the blue color at the ureteral orifices occurs within 4 to 9 minutes after intravenous injection of indigotindisulfonate sodium.
