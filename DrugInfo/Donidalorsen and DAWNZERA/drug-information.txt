    General Information
    Anti-drug antibody formation has been reported with donidalorsen. The incidence of treatment-emergent anti-drug antibodies (ADAs) was 20% (9 of 45 subjects) for the donidalorsen 80 mg every 4 weeks group and 22% (5 of 23 subjects) for the donidalorsen 80 mg every 8 weeks group in a hereditary angioedema (HAE) clinical trial. Description
  Text
  Donidalorsen is a prekallikrein-directed antisense oligonucleotide indicated for prophylaxis to prevent attacks of hereditary angioedema (HAE). Donidalorsen lowers PKK concentration and prevents excessive bradykinin production in individuals with HAE. PKK is a pro-enzyme for plasma kallikrein that results in the release of bradykinin, a potent vasodilator causing swelling and pain in hereditary angioedema (HAE). C1-inhibitor (C1-INH) deficiency or dysfunction leads to excessive plasma kallikrein activity, bradykinin generation, and angioedema attacks in individuals with HAE. Mild, non-serious injection site reaction was the most common adverse event reported with donidalorsen in clinical trials. The development of anti-drug antibodies (ADAs) may occur with donidalorsen use; however, the development of ADAs against donidalorsen did not affect the pharmacokinetics, safety, or efficacy of donidalorsen in clinical trials. Donidalorsen was FDA-approved in August 2025.

Mechanism Of Action
  Text
  Donidalorsen is an antisense oligonucleotide N-acetyl galactosamine (ASO-GalNac) conjugate that causes ribonuclease H1 (RNase H1)-mediated degradation of PKK mRNA. PKK mRNA degradation occurs by donidalorsen binding to PKK mRNA and results in decreased production of PKK protein. PKK is a pro-enzyme for plasma kallikrein that results in the release of bradykinin, a potent vasodilator causing swelling and pain in hereditary angioedema (HAE). C1-inhibitor (C1-INH) deficiency or dysfunction leads to excessive plasma kallikrein activity, bradykinin generation, and angioedema attacks in individuals with HAE. Donidalorsen lowers PKK concentration and prevents excessive bradykinin production in individuals with HAE.
Pharmacodynamics
In donidalorsen clinical trials, a decrease in plasma PKK concentrations was observed at the first assessment (Week 4) after treatment with donidalorsen 80mg. The mean percentage reduction from baseline at Week 4 across both treatment groups was 48%. The mean percentage change from baseline to Week 24 in trough plasma PKK concentrations indicated reductions of 73% and 47% after treatment with donidalorsen 80 mg every 4 weeks and 80 mg every 8 weeks, respectively, compared with a slight increase (2%) in the placebo group.

  Text
  Donidalorsen is administered subcutaneously. The donidalorsen apparent volumes of distribution for the central (Vc/F) and peripheral (Vp/F) compartment were 69.8 L and 1,840 L, respectively. Donidalorsen is expected to distribute primarily to the liver and kidney cortex after subcutaneous dosing. Donidalorsen is highly protein bound to human plasma proteins (more than 98%) in vitro. The oligonucleotide moiety of donidalorsen is expected to be metabolized by endo- and exonucleases to short oligonucleotide fragments of varying sizes within the liver. The linker that covalently connects the antisense oligonucleotide (ASO) to the N-acetyl galactosamine (GalNAc) residues is cleaved by hydrolysis and undergoes dephosphorylation and subsequent oxidative metabolism to form inactive metabolites, which are minimally released in circulation. The most abundant linker-related metabolite (M8) is a substrate of CYP3A4. The mean fraction of unchanged ASO eliminated in urine was less than 1% of the administered dose in healthy subjects within 24 hours post-dose. The renal route of elimination is minor for linker-related metabolites. The terminal plasma elimination half-life of donidalorsen in a typical individual with hereditary angioedema (HAE) is approximately 1 month. The half-life of the initial rapid clearance phase, reflecting tissue distribution, was approximately 5 hours.

    Administration Details
      Name
      Subcutaneous Administration
      Description
      Remove the autoinjector from the refrigerator 30 minutes prior to administration.
Keep the autoinjector in the original carton and allow the autoinjector to come to room temperature for 30 minutes before injecting.
Do not try to speed up the warming process using other heat sources (e.g., microwave, hot water).
Choose the injection site on the stomach or the front of the thigh. Health care providers or caregivers may choose the back of the upper arm as the injection site.
Do not inject within 2 inches of the navel, into skin that is bruised, tender, red, or hard, or into scars or damaged skin.
Clean the injection site with an alcohol wipe and let the skin air dry. Do not touch the cleaned skin before injecting.
Hold the autoinjector by the middle with the clear cap facing away.
Remove the clear cap by pulling it straight off. Do not twist it off. The needle is inside the orange needle shield.
Do not remove the clear cap until right before injection.
Do not recap the autoinjector. Do not push the orange needle shield against the hand or finger.
Hold the autoinjector in 1 hand. Place the orange needle shield at a 90-degree angle against the skin. Ensure the viewing window can be seen.
Push firmly and hold the autoinjector straight against the skin. A click will be heard as the injection starts.
A second click may be heard. The injection is not finished.
Hold the autoinjector against the skin for 10 seconds to ensure the full dose has been administered.
Do not move, turn, or change the angle of the autoinjector during the injection.
Verify that the orange plunger rod has moved down to fill the entire viewing window.
If the orange plunger rod does not fill the viewing window, the full dose may not have been administered.
Remove the autoinjector by lifting it straight up. After removal from the skin, the orange needle shield locks in place and covers the needle.
The autoinjector contains 1 dose. Do not reuse the autoinjector or clear cap.
Dispose of the autoinjector in a sharps container. Do not throw away the autoinjector in the trash.

    Visually inspect parenteral products for particulate matter and discoloration prior to administration whenever solution and container permit.

Storage: Donidalorsen may be stored at room temperature up to 86 degrees F (30 degrees C) in the original carton for up to 6 weeks. If not used within the 6 weeks stored at room temperature, discard it. Do not freeze. Do not expose to heat. Protect from direct light.
