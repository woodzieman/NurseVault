        Dosage Description
        0.045 mg/kg IV every 3 weeks until disease progression or unacceptable toxicity. Administer with the recommended premedications. Coadministration of certain drugs may need to be avoided or dosage adjustments may be necessary; review drug interactions. Therapy interruption, dose reduction, and/or discontinuation may be necessary if toxicity occurs. The rate of complete remission (CR; 48.5%) or clinical complete remission (CRc; 21.2%) was 69.7% in adults with treatment-naive (n = 33) BPDCN with no central nervous system involvement who received pivekimab sunirine in a clinical trial (CADENZA). At a median follow-up of 21.5 months, the median time to CR/CRc was 1.8 months and the median duration of CR/CRc was 9.7 months. In adults with relapsed or refractory BPDCN (n = 51) who received pivekimab sunirine in this trial, the CR (13.7%) or CRc (2%) rate was 15.7%. At a median follow-up of 24.1 months, the median time to CR/CRc was 1.7 months and the median duration of CR/CRc was 9.2 months.

    Headline
    Hypersensitivity
    General Information
    This medication is contraindicated in patients with a history of hypersensitivity to it or any of its components.
    Headline
    hepatic disease, hepatotoxicity, sinusoidal obstruction syndrome
    General Information
    Severe or life-threatening hepatotoxicity including sinusoidal obstruction syndrome (SOS), formerly known as hepatic veno-occlusive disease (VOD), has been reported with pivekimab sunirine therapy. The risk of SOS may be greater in patients who receive a hematopoietic stem-cell transplant (HSCT); use with caution in patients with hepatic disease. Avoid use in patients with moderate to severe hepatic impairment (total bilirubin greater than 1.5 times ULN with any AST). Monitor liver function tests prior to each pivekimab sunirine dose. Therapy interruption may be necessary in patients who develop liver test abnormalities. Closely monitor patients for signs and symptoms of SOS including hepatomegaly, rapid weight gain, and ascites. Permanently discontinue therapy in patients who develop SOS; treat according to standard medical practice.
    Headline
    renal failure, renal impairment
    General Information
    Avoid use of pivekimab sunirine in patients with moderate to severe renal impairment (CrCl less than 60 mL/minute) or end stage renal disease/renal failure. A higher incidence of grade 3 or higher adverse events, serious adverse events, and dose delays was observed in patients with moderate renal impairment (CrCl 30 to less than 60 mL/minute).
    Headline
    asthma
    General Information
    Use pivekimab sunirine with caution in patients with sulfite sensitivity, which is seen more frequently in individuals with asthma. Pivekimab sunirine contains sodium metabisulfite, a sulfite that may cause allergic type reactions, including anaphylactic symptoms and life-threatening or less severe asthmatic episodes in certain susceptible individuals.
    Headline
    pregnancy
    General Information
    Avoid the use of pivekimab sunirine during pregnancy. Pivekimab sunirine may cause fetal harm if administered during pregnancy based on its mechanism of action because it contains a genotoxic compound, FGN849, and affects actively dividing cells. There are no available data on pivekimab sunirine use during human pregnancy to inform a drug-associated risk; animal reproductive or developmental toxicity studies were not conducted.
    Headline
    people who can cause pregnancy in others, people who may become pregnant, reproductive risk
    General Information
    Counsel people who may become pregnant and people who can cause pregnancy in others about the reproductive risk associated with pivekimab sunirine therapy. Pivekimab sunirine may cause fetal harm. Verify pregnancy status prior to treatment initiation. Advise people who may become pregnant to use effective contraception while taking pivekimab sunirine and for 7 months after the last dose. For those that can cause pregnancy in others, contraception should be used during pivekimab sunirine treatment and for 4 months after the last dose. Based on animal data, pivekimab sunirine may cause male and female infertility.
    Headline
    breast-feeding
    General Information
    Advise individuals to avoid breast-feeding during pivekimab sunirine treatment and for 1 month after the final dose due to the potential for serious adverse reactions in the breastfed child. There are no data on the presence of pivekimab sunirine or its metabolites in human milk, the effects on the breastfed child, or the effects on milk production.

Adverse Reactions
  Headline
  Adverse Reactions
  Adverse Reactions
    Headline
    elevated hepatic enzymes, hepatotoxicity, hyperbilirubinemia, sinusoidal obstruction syndrome, veno-occlusive disease
    General Information
    Severe or life-threatening hepatotoxicity including sinusoidal obstruction syndrome (SOS), formerly known as hepatic veno-occlusive disease (VOD), was reported in 2% of patients during pivekimab sunirine treatment in a clinical trial, with an onset up to 30 days after the last dose; 6% of patients experienced VOD/SOS during treatment or following a subsequent hematopoietic stem cell transplantation (HSCT). Among the patients who proceeded to HSCT, SOS/VOD occurred (26%), including 2 fatal cases. The median time from subsequent HSCT to onset of SOS/VOD was 11 days. Therapy interruption, dose reduction, or permanent discontinuation may be necessary in patients who develop hepatotoxicity. Elevated hepatic enzymes, including increased ALT (32%; grade 3 or 4, 4.4%), AST (29%; grade 3 or 4, 0.9%), and alkaline phosphatase (20%; grade 3 or 4, 0.9%) levels, and hyperbilirubinemia (16%; grade 3 or 4, 0.9%) occurred in patients treated with pivekimab sunirine in a clinical trial.

Description
  Text
  Pivekimab sunirine is a CD123-directed antibody and alkylating agent conjugate indicated for the treatment of adults with blastic plasmacytoid dendritic cell neoplasm (BPDCN). Severe or life-threatening hepatotoxicity, including veno-occlusive disease/sinusoidal obstruction syndrome, has been reported. Pivekimab sunirine was approved by the FDA in May 2026.

Mechanism Of Action
  Text
  Pivekimab sunirine is a CD123-directed antibody-drug conjugate (ADC). The antibody is a humanized anti-CD123 IgG1. Pivekimab sunirine binds to CD123 expressing cells and upon intracellular processing releases a cell membrane-permeable payload, FGN849, leading to DNA alkylation, single-strand DNA breaks, apoptosis, and cell death. The payload, FGN849, is a member of the indolinobenzodiazepine pseudodimer (IGN) class of cytotoxic molecules. Pivekimab sunirine has exhibited antitumor activity in in vitro and in vivo models of blastic plasmacytoid dendritic cell neoplasm (BPDCN).

  Text
  Pivekimab sunirine is administered intravenously. It is a CD123-directed antibody-drug conjugate (ADC) consisting of an IgG1 monoclonal antibody and an alkylating agent payload, FGN849. Pivekimab sunirine has a mean volume of distribution of 5.4 L (CV, 39%), a mean clearance of 1.8 L/hour (CV, 76%), and an elimination half-life of approximately 1.5 hours. FGN849 plasma protein binding is 99.6% in vitro. Pivekimab sunirine is metabolized into small peptides and amino acids via catabolic pathways. FGN849 is primarily metabolized by CYP3A.
Pharmacodynamics: Higher FGN849 exposure was associated with increased rates of grade 2 or higher infusion-related reactions.
 
Affected cytochrome P450 isoenzymes and drug transporters: CYP3A,CYP2C8, P-gp, BCRP
FGN849 is primarily a substrate of CYP3A. It is also an inhibitor of CYP3A and CYP2C8 and a substrate of P-gp and BCRP. Concomitant use of pivekimab sunirine with strong and moderate CYP3A inhibitors may increase unconjugated FGN849 exposure.

      Name
      Intravenous Administration
      Description
      Calculate the dose based on the patient's actual body weight.
Pivekimab sunirine requires reconstitution followed by two dilutions prior to administration.
Reconstitution
Calculate the dose and the number of vials required.
Reconstitute each vial with 1.1 mL of Sterile Water for injection for a final vial concentration of 2 mg/mL.
Gently swirl the vial in a circular motion; do not shake.
The reconstituted solution should be clear to slightly opalescent, colorless to slightly yellow, and free of visible contaminants.
Storage following reconstitution: Store for up to 4 hours refrigerated (2 to 8 degrees C; 36 to 46 degrees F); do not freeze.
First Dilution
Add 4.8 mL of 5% Dextrose injection to the reconstituted solution in the vial.
Mix by gentle inversion; do not shake.
The resulting diluted solution concentration is 0.4 mg/mL.
Calculate the required administration volume needed from the first dilution of vial(s).
Pivekimab sunirine requires a second dilution prior to administration.
Second Dilution
Withdraw the calculated volume from the first dilution of vial(s); discard any unused drug remaining.
Withdraw an equal volume of 5% Dextrose injection into a separate syringe.
Transfer the first dilution solution into the syringe containing 5% Dextrose injection; draw air into the syringe and close.
Gently invert the syringe to mix the solution; do not shake.
Remove air bubbles from the syringe before administration; final concentration is 0.2 mg/mL.
Do not use an in-line filter due to increased drug loss. If an in-line filter is required, a 1.2 micron polyethersulfone (PES) filter may be used.
Storage of diluted solution: Store refrigerated (2 to 8 degrees C; 36 to 46 degrees F) for no more than 24 hours, including 8 hours at room temperature (9 to 25 degrees C; 48 to 77 degrees F), from the time of reconstitution to completion of the infusion. Do not freeze or expose to light. If refrigerated, allow solution to come to room temperature prior to administration (approximately 30 minutes); do not shake.
Intravenous (IV) Infusion Using Syringe Administration
Protect the IV syringe from light using a light-blocking cover during infusion; the infusion line does not need to be protected from light.
Administer as an IV infusion as follows: First infusion, at a rate of 0.8 mL/minute (0.165 mg/minute) for the first 30 minutes; may increase rate after 30 minutes to 1.7 mL/minute (0.33 mg/minute), if tolerated. Subsequent infusions may be delivered at the highest tolerated rate.
Flush the IV line following the infusion with sufficient volume of 5% Dextrose injection to ensure delivery of the full dose.
Do not mix with any other drugs or IV fluids other than 5% Dextrose injection.
Monitor closely for infusion-related reactions during the infusion and for at least 4 hours or longer after the first infusion and for at least 1 hour after subsequent infusions.

Dosage Adjustment Guidelines
  Hepatic Impairment
  Baseline hepatic impairment
Mild (total bilirubin ULN or less and AST greater than ULN or total bilirubin 1.5 times ULN or less): No dosage adjustment needed.
Moderate or severe (total bilirubin greater than 1.5 times ULN with any AST): Avoid pivekimab sunirine use.
Treatment-related hepatotoxicity
AST or ALT level greater than 2.5 times ULN: Delay treatment until AST or ALT levels have returned to 2.5 times ULN or less.
Total bilirubin level greater than 1.5 times ULN: Delay treatment until level has returned to 1.5 times ULN or less.
  Renal Impairment
  Baseline renal impairment
Mild (CrCl 60 to less than 90 mL/minute): No dosage adjustment needed.
Moderate or severe (CrCl less than 60 mL/minute) or end stage renal disease: Avoid pivekimab sunirine use.
