    Headline
    helminth infection
    General Information
    Treat individuals with a pre-existing helminth infection prior to initiating therapy with tezepelumab. If an individual becomes infected while receiving tezepelumab therapy and does not respond to anti-helminth treatment, discontinue tezepelumab therapy until the infection resolves. Thymic stromal lymphopoietin (TSLP) may be involved in the immunological response to some helminth infections. Although it is not known whether if tezepelumab will influence the body's response against parasitic infections, caution is warranted. Individuals with known parasitic infections were excluded from clinical trials.
    Headline
    pregnancy
    General Information
    There are no available data regarding tezepelumab use during human pregnancy to evaluate for any drug-associated risk of major birth defects, miscarriage, or other adverse maternal or fetal outcomes. Monoclonal antibodies, such as tezepelumab, are transported across the placenta as pregnancy progresses; therefore, potential fetal effects are likely to be greater during the third trimester. In a prenatal and postnatal development study conducted in cynomolgus monkeys, there was no evidence of fetal harm with intravenous tezepelumab administration throughout pregnancy at drug exposures approximately 168 times the exposure at the maximum recommended human dose (MRHD) of 210 mg administered subcutaneously. Uncontrolled asthma also presents risks during pregnancy. In individuals with poorly or moderately controlled asthma, there is an increased risk of preeclampsia in the mother and prematurity, low birth weight, and small for gestational age in the neonate. Asthma control should be closely monitored during pregnancy and treatment adjusted to maintain optimal control. There is a pregnancy exposure registry that monitors outcomes in pregnant individuals exposed to tezepelumab; information about the registry can be obtained at mothertobaby.org/pregnancy-studies/ or by calling 1-877-311-8972.
    Headline
    breast-feeding
    General Information
    Use tezepelumab with caution during breast-feeding. Asthma biologics may be initiated or continued while breast-feeding if the risks and benefits have been discussed, and the patient is in agreement. There is no information regarding the presence of tezepelumab in human milk, the effects on the breastfed infant, or the effects on milk production. Tezepelumab is a humanized monoclonal antibody (IgG2 lambda), and immunoglobulin G (IgG) is present in human milk in small amounts.     antibody formation
    General Information
    Anti-tezepelumab antibody formation occurred in 29 (5%) out of 601 subjects who received tezepelumab for asthma at the recommended dosing regimen during the 48 to 52-week study period. Of these 29 subjects, 11 subjects (2% of subjects treated with tezepelumab) developed treatment-emergent antibodies and 1 subject (less than 1% of subjects treated with tezepelumab) developed neutralizing antibodies. Neutralizing antibodies were detected in 1 of the ADA positive subjects. While there was no observed impact of ADA on pharmacokinetics, pharmacodynamics, efficacy, or safety, there were insufficient numbers of subjects with treatment-emergent ADA to make a formal assessment in CRSwNP.

Description
  Text
  Tezepelumab is a subcutaneously administered thymic stromal lymphopoietin (TSLP) blocker, human monoclonal antibody (IgG2 lambda), indicated for the add-on maintenance treatment of severe asthma and chronic rhinosinusitis with nasal polyps (CRSwNP) in adult and pediatric individuals 12 years and older. Individuals receive an injection once every 4 weeks. Tezepelumab does not have a phenotype (e.g. eosinophilic or allergic) or biomarker limitation. Guidelines identify tezepelumab as an add-on anti-TSLP option for individuals 12 years and older with severe asthma that remains uncontrolled despite optimized high-dose inhaled corticosteroid (ICS) and long-acting beta-agonist (LABA) therapy, or that requires maintenance oral corticosteroids (OCS), after modifiable factors are addressed. In both the PATHWAY trial and NAVIGATOR trials, subjects with asthma treated with tezepelumab for 52 weeks had significant reductions in the annualized rate of asthma exacerbations compared to placebo. The annualized asthma exacerbation rate ratios for PATHWAY and NAVIGATOR were 0.29, CI 0.16 to 0.51 and 0.44, CI 0.37 to 0.53, respectively. There were also fewer exacerbations requiring emergency room visits and/or hospitalization in subjects treated with tezepelumab compared with placebo. In the WAYPOINT trial, statistically significant efficacy was observed with tezepelumab for the improvement in total nasal polyp score [NPS; -2.01 (CI -2.33 to -1.68)] and in bi-weekly mean nasal congestion score [NCS; -0.95 (CI -1.12 to -0.78)] in subjects with CRSwNP. Tezepelumab significantly reduced the proportion of subjects with need for sino-nasal surgery or systemic corticosteroids by 92% compared to placebo over 52 weeks (HR: 0.08, CI 0.03 to 0.17). The proportion of subjects requiring sino-nasal surgery or systemic corticosteroids were reduced by 98% (HR: 0.02, CI 0 to 0.09) and 88% (HR: 0.12, CI 0.04 to 0.27), respectively, over 52 weeks compared to placebo. Additionally, loss of smell significantly improved with a decrease in Lund-Mackay (LMK) sinus CT scan score compared to placebo [-1.01 (CI -1.18 to -0.83) and -5.76 (CI: -6.45 to -5.07), respectively]. Tezepelumab-ekko was FDA approved in December 2021.

Mechanism Of Action
  Text
  Tezepelumab is a thymic stromal lymphopoietin (TSLP) blocker, human monoclonal antibody immunoglobulin G2 (IgG2) lambda that binds to human TSLP with a dissociation constant of 15.8 picomolar (pM) and blocks its interaction with the heterodimeric TSLP receptor. TSLP is a cytokine mainly derived from epithelial cells and occupies an upstream position in inflammatory cascades. Increased levels of TSLP and mRNA and protein are found in the airways of individuals with asthma as well as in nasal polyp tissue. By blocking TSLP, tezepelumab reduces biomarkers and cytokines associated with inflammation including blood eosinophils, airway submucosal eosinophils, IgE, fractional exhaled nitric oxide (FeNO), interleukin (IL)-5, and IL-13; however, the mechanism of tezepelumab action in asthma and chronic rhinosinusitis with nasal polyps (CRSwNP) have not been definitively established.

  Text
  Tezepelumab is administered by subcutaneous injection. The central and peripheral Vd of tezepelumab were 3.9 L and 2.2 L, respectively, for a 70-kg individual. Tezepelumab is a humanized IgG2 monoclonal antibody that is degraded by proteolytic enzymes widely distributed in the body and not restricted to hepatic tissue. Tezepelumab exhibited dose-proportional pharmacokinetics and no evidence of target receptor-mediated clearance pathway in a population pharmacokinetic analysis. The estimated typical systemic clearance for tezepelumab was 0.17 L/day for a subject weighing 70 kg. Following subcutaneous administration, the elimination half-life was approximately 26 days.
 
Affected cytochrome P450 isoenzymes and drug transporters: none
Hepatic CYP450 isoenzymes, efflux pumps, and protein-binding mechanisms are not involved in the clearance of tezepelumab. An effect of tezepelumab on the pharmacokinetics of co-administered medications is not expected. Based on the population analysis, commonly co-administered medications had no effect on tezepelumab clearance.

      Description
      Each vial, prefilled syringe, and prefilled pen contains a single dose of tezepelumab and is for single-use only.
Prior to administration, warm the vial, prefilled syringe, or prefilled pen by leaving carton at room temperature for about 60 minutes; do not expose to heat. Do not shake.

Do not put back in the refrigerator once the carton has reached room temperature. Keep in the original carton to protect from light. Do not expose to heat. Dispose of the injection properly if left out of the refrigerator in the unopened carton for more than 30 days.
 
Preparation of a Prefilled Syringe
Do not touch the needle guard activation clips to prevent premature activation of the needle safety guard. Grasp the syringe body, not the plunger, to remove prefilled syringe from the carton.
Do not remove the needle cover until ready to inject. Hold the syringe body and remove the needle cover by pulling straight off. Be careful not to hold the plunger or plunger head while removing the needle cover or the plunger may move. You may see a drop of liquid at the end of the needle; this is normal.
If the prefilled syringe is damaged or contaminated (for example, dropped without needle cover in place), discard and use a new prefilled syringe.
 
Subcutaneous Administration of the Prefilled Syringe
Gently pinch the skin and insert the needle at approximately a 45-degree angle into the recommended injection site (i.e., upper arm, thigh, or abdomen, except for the 5 cm around the navel). Do not inject into areas where the skin is tender, bruised, erythematous, or hardened.
Rotate injection sites with each injection.
Inject all the medication by pushing in the plunger all the way until the plunger head is completely between the needle guard activation clips. This is necessary to activate the needle guard.
After injection, maintain pressure on the plunger head and remove the needle from the skin. Release pressure on the plunger head to allow the needle guard to cover the needle. Do not re-cap the prefilled syringe.
Discard the used syringe into a proper sharps disposal container.
 
Subcutaneous Administration of the Prefilled Pen
Injection may be given by either gently pinching the skin at the injection site or giving the injection without pinching the skin. Inject into the thigh or abdomen, except for the 5 cm around the navel. A patient should not attempt to inject into the upper arm. Do not inject into areas where the skin is tender, bruised, erythematous, or hardened.
Rotate injection sites with each injection.
When injecting, the first click indicates that the injection has started. Press and hold the prefilled pen for 15 seconds until you hear the second click. Do not change the position of the prefilled pen once the injection has started.
After injection, lift the prefilled pen straight up allowing the orange needle guard to slide down and lock into place over the needle.
Discard the used pen into a proper sharps disposal container.
