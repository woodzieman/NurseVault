Drug Class: Sebetralstat is an oral plasma kallikrein inhibitor indicated for the treatment of acute attacks of hereditary angioedema (HAE).

Routes: oral; Common adult doses: 1,200 mg/24 hours PO.

In an Overdose: No overdose/antidote information is listed in this file.

Key Nursing Considerations: Contraindications/precautions: This medication is contraindicated in patients with a history of hypersensitivity to it or any of its components. Child-Pugh class B, Child-Pugh class C, hepatic failure Avoid use of sebetralstat in individuals with severe hepatic impairment or hepatic failure (Child-Pugh class C).

    Child-Pugh class B, Child-Pugh class C, hepatic failure
    General Information
    Avoid use of sebetralstat in individuals with severe hepatic impairment or hepatic failure (Child-Pugh class C). A reduced sebetralstat dose is required in individuals with moderate hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh class B).
    Headline
    pregnancy
    General Information
    There are no available data on the use of sebetralstat in pregnancy to evaluate for a drug-associated risk of major birth defects, miscarriage, or other adverse maternal or fetal outcomes. In animal reproduction studies, oral administration of sebetralstat to pregnant rats and rabbits during organogenesis did not produce evidence of fetal harm with dose exposures up to approximately 15 and 11 times, respectively, the human exposure at the maximum recommended human dose (MRHD) of up to 1,200 mg (on an area under the curve [AUC] basis). Sebetralstat did produce an increase in embryofetal losses and fetal malformations in rats at an exposure of 60 times the MRHD.
    Headline
    breast-feeding
    General Information
    Consider the developmental and health benefits of breast-feeding along with the mother's clinical need for sebetralstat and any potential adverse effects on the breastfed child from sebetralstat or from the underlying maternal condition. There are no data on the presence of sebetralstat or its metabolites in human milk, the effects on the breastfed infant, or the effects on milk production. Radioactivity is present in animal milk after dosing with radiolabeled sebetralstat. When drug is present in animal milk, it is likely that the drug will be present in human milk. Data in rats have shown excretion of sebetralstat and/or its metabolites in milk.

Description
  Text
  Sebetralstat is an oral plasma kallikrein inhibitor indicated for the treatment of acute attacks of hereditary angioedema (HAE). Plasma kallikrein is a serine protease that cleaves high molecular weight kininogen (HK) releasing bradykinin, which increases vascular permeability through activation of bradykinin receptors causing edema. Sebetralstat treats the clinical symptoms of acute attacks of HAE by inhibiting the cleavage of HK and reducing the production of bradykinin. Headache was the most common adverse event reported with sebetralstat in clinical trials. Sebetralstat was FDA-approved in July 2025.

Mechanism Of Action
  Text
  Sebetralstat is a competitive, reversible inhibitor of plasma kallikrein. Plasma kallikrein is a serine protease that cleaves high molecular weight kininogen (HK) releasing bradykinin, which increases vascular permeability through activation of bradykinin receptors causing edema. Sebetralstat treats the clinical symptoms of acute attacks of hereditary angioedema (HAE) by inhibiting the cleavage of HK and reducing the production of bradykinin. Sebetralstat also inhibits the positive feedback mechanism of the kallikrein kinin system by plasma kallikrein which causes a reduction of factor Xlla and additional plasma kallikrein generation.
Pharmacodynamics
Concentration-dependent inhibition of plasma kallikrein, measured as a reduction from baseline of specific enzyme activity, was observed in exploratory assays. After administration of a single sebetralstat 600 mg oral dose to healthy subjects, more than 90% inhibition of plasma kallikrein activity was observed starting at 30 minutes post-dose and maintained through approximately 6 hours post-dose.

  Sebetralstat is administered orally. The estimated typical apparent volume of distribution (Vz/F) for sebetralstat is 70.1 L. Plasma protein binding of sebetralstat is 77% in vitro.
