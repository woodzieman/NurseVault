    General Information
    Treatment with ensifentrine has been associated with an increase in psychiatric adverse reactions, including suicide-related adverse reactions. One patient who received ensifentrine in a pooled 24-week safety population sexperienced a suicide-related adverse reaction (suicide attempt). In another controlled study, 1 patient who received ensifentrine experienced a suicide-related adverse reaction (suicide). If psychiatric events occur during ensifentrine treatment, evaluate risks and benefits of treatment continuation.

Description
  Text
  Ensifentrine is a phosphodiesterase-3 (PDE3) inhibitor and phosphodiesterase-4 (PDE4) inhibitor inhalation that is delivered via nebulization. It is indicated for the maintenance treatment of chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD) in adults. PDE3 and PDE4 inhibition results in intracellular accumulation of cyclic adenosine monophosphate (cAMP) and/or cyclic guanosine monophosphate (cGMP), a process that appears to down-regulate inflammation. The efficacy of ensifentrine was assessed in two 24-week, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trials, ENHANCE-1 and ENHANCE-2. These studies enrolled 1,553 adults with moderate to severe COPD. Both trials demonstrated that ensifentrine significantly improved lung function as measured by the change from baseline in FEV-1 AUC0-12h at Week 12 compared to placebo. Additionally, health-related quality of life was assessed using the St. George's Respiratory Questionnaire (SGRQ). ENHANCE-1 reported a significant improvement in SGRQ scores with ensifentrine compared to placebo, while ENHANCE-2 showed no significant difference between the treatment and placebo. Ensifentrine may be considered as add-on maintenance therapy in individuals with COPD who remain symptomatic, particularly with persistent dyspnea, despite treatment with long-acting bronchodilators. Ensifentrine is not recommended as initial COPD therapy or for the treatment of acute COPD exacerbations. Ensifentrine was FDA-approved in 2024.

Mechanism Of Action
  Text
  Ensifentrine inhibits phosphodiesterase-3 (PDE3) and phosphodiesterase-4 (PDE4) enzymes. PDE3 primarily hydrolyzes the second-messenger molecule cyclic adenosine monophosphate (cAMP) but is also capable of hydrolyzing cyclic guanosine monophosphate (cGMP). PDE4 hydrolyzes cAMP only. Inhibition of PDE3 and PDE4 results in accumulation of intracellular levels of cAMP and/or cGMP, resulting in various downstream signalling effects.

    Description
    After inhalation, ensifentrine Cmax was attained around 0.6 to 1.5 hours after dosing in both healthy individuals and COPD patients. At 3 times the recommended dosage, exposure to ensifentrine increases by 1.4 times. Population pharmacokinetic analysis predicts accumulation of ensifentrine of 1.3 and 1.4-fold for Cmax and AUC in healthy subjects and 1.4 and 1.5-fold for Cmax and AUC in subjects with COPD. The drug demonstrates high inter-subject variability in exposure. About 90% of the inhaled dose is delivered to the lung, and the relative bioavailability of ensifentrine is approximately 35% lower in COPD patients compared to healthy individuals.

  Text
  Ensifentrine is administered via oral inhalation using nebulization. Plasma protein binding is approximately 90% in vitro. In healthy subjects, the apparent volume of distribution is 2,700 L centrally and 1,820 L peripherally, while in COPD patients, these values are higher at 8,150 L and 5,490 L, respectively. Steady state is achieved by day 3 with twice-daily dosing. The drug undergoes primary metabolism through hydroxylation and O-demethylation, followed by glucuronidation, mainly involving CYP2C9 and to a lesser extent CYP2D6. Following a single nebulized dose (8 times the recommended dose), unchanged ensifentrine comprises 96% to 99% of the drug-related material in plasma. The terminal elimination half-life ranges from 10.6 to 12.6 hours. Excretion occurs primarily via feces, with minimal unchanged drug excreted in urine.
Affected cytochrome P450 (CYP450) isoenzymes and drug transporters: CYP2C9, CYP2D6
Because ensifentrine is primarily metabolized by CYP2C9, concomitant administration of a CYP2C9 inhibitor can increase plasma concentrations and exposure of ensifentrine. Ensifentrine is a substrate of BCRP. Ensifentrine is not a substrate of P-gp, OATP1B1, OATP1B3. At therapeutically relevant concentrations, ensifentrine does not inhibit BRCP, CYP1A2, CYP2B6, CYP2C8, CYP2C9, CYP2C19, CYP2D6, CYP3A4, MATE1, MATE2-K, OATP1B1, OATP1B3, OAT1, OAT3, OCT2, or P-gp.

  Route Specific Administrations
    Administration Details
      Name
      Oral Inhalation Administration
      Description
      Nebulized inhalation suspension (Ohtuvayre):
Administer by oral inhalation via nebulizer only; do not swallow.
One ampule contains 3 mg of ensifentrine.
Only remove the unit-dose ampule from foil pouch immediately before use.
Shake the ampule vigorously. The contents will appear clousy and yellow to pale yellow in color.
Squeeze and completely empty the contents of the ampule into the nebulizer cup for administration by oral inhalation.
Administer by oral inhalation using a standard jet nebulizer equipped with a mouthpiece, connected to an air compressor.
Do not mix with other drugs. The compatibility of ensifentrine inhalation solution with other drugs administered by nebulization has not been established.
Deliver suspension by nebulization over about 5 to 7 minutes.
Discard ampule and any residual suspension after use.

Dosage Adjustment Guidelines
  Hepatic Impairment
  Use ensifentrine with caution in patients with hepatic impairment. Specific guidelines for dosage adjustments in hepatic impairment are not available. Ensifentrine systemic exposure increased by 2.3-fold in subjects with moderate or severe hepatic impairment compared with healthy subjects.

  No dosage adjustments in patients with mild or moderate renal impairment is required. Patients with severe renal impairment have not been evaluated.
