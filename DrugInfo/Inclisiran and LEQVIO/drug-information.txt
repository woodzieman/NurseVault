    Headline
    pregnancy
    General Information
    Discontinue inclisiran when pregnancy is recognized. Because of insufficient human pregnancy data, guidelines recommend avoiding inclisiran use during pregnancy. FDA-approved labeling advises considering the individual's ongoing need for therapy, particularly in those at high risk for cardiovascular events during pregnancy, such as patients with atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease or familial hypercholesterolemia; however, this consideration is not addressed in current guidelines. Inclisiran may cause fetal harm based on its mechanism of action. Inclisiran increases LDL-C uptake and lowers LDL-C concentrations in the circulation, which decreases cholesterol and possibly other biologically active substances derived from cholesterol. Cholesterol and other products of the cholesterol biosynthesis pathway are essential components for fetal development, including synthesis of steroids and cell membranes. In animal reproduction studies, no adverse developmental effects were seen when inclisiran was given to rats and rabbits at doses 5 and 10 times, respectively, the maximum recommended human dose (MRHD) based on body surface area comparison during organogenesis through lactation. Temporary discontinuation of lipid-lowering therapy should have minimal impact on the long-term therapy of primary hypercholesterolemia for most patients, as atherosclerosis is a chronic process. There is a pregnancy safety study for individuals exposed to inclisiran during pregnancy to monitor pregnancy outcomes. Report pregnancies to Novartis at 1-888-669-6682 or usdrugsafety.operations@novartis.com.
    Headline
    breast-feeding
    General Information
    Use inclisiran with caution during breast-feeding. Some guidelines recommend avoidance during lactation due to a lack of data. Inclisiran is present in the milk of lactating rats. Oligonucleotide-based products typically have poor bioavailability; therefore, it is unlikely that low concentrations of inclisiran present in milk will adversely affect a child's development during breast-feeding. Consider the benefits of breast-feeding, the patient's clinical need for treatment, and any potential adverse effects on the breastfed child from the medication or from the patient's underlying medical condition.

    antibody formation
    General Information
    Antibody formation may occur with inclisiran use. During clinical trials (n = 1,830 adult patients), confirmed positivity for anti-drug antibodies was detected in 33 (2%) patients prior to dosing and in 90 (5%) patients during 18 months of treatment. Approximately 31 (2%) inclisiran-treated patients with a negative sample at baseline had a persistent anti-drug antibody response, defined as 2 confirmed positive samples separated by at least 16 weeks or a single confirmed final sample. In pediatric patients with heterozygous familial hypercholesterolemia (HeFH), confirmed positivity was not detected in Year 1, but was detected in 8/139 (6%) of patients during Year 2. In pediatric patients with homozygous familial hypercholesterolemia (HoFH), confirmed positivity was detected in 1/13 (8%) patients at pre-dose and throughout the trial. There was no clinically significant evidence that the presence of anti-drug binding antibodies affected the pharmacodynamic profile, clinical response, or safety of inclisiran over the treatment period. However, the long-term consequences of continuing inclisiran treatment in the presence of anti-drug antibodies are unknown.

Description
  Text
  Inclisiran is a parenteral small interfering RNA (siRNA) directed to PCSK9 (proprotein convertase subtilisin/kexin type 9) mRNA indicated for use as an adjunct to diet and exercise for the treatment of hypercholesterolemia, heterozygous familial hypercholesterolemia (HeFH), and homozygous familial hypercholesterolemia (HoFH) to reduce low-density lipoprotein cholesterol (LDL-C). Inclisiran prevents PCSK9 translation in the liver, which increases LDL-C uptake through increases in LDL-receptor recycling and expression on hepatocyte cells. In clinical trials of adults with HeFH or clinical ASCVD with elevated LDL-C while receiving maximally tolerated statin therapy, inclisiran resulted in sustained LDL-C reduction of 48% to 52% compared to placebo. During clinical trials in pediatric patients with HeFH or HoFH, inclisiran reduced LDL-C versus placebo by 29% and 33%, respectively; however, the HoFH result was derived from a small, descriptive trial that was not powered for statistical testing. Inclisiran may be associated with the development of anti-drug antibodies; however, there has been no clinically significant effect of anti-inclisiran antibodies on pharmacodynamics, safety, or effectiveness of inclisiran over the treatment duration of 18 months. Inclisiran was FDA-approved in 2021.

Mechanism Of Action
  Text
  Inclisiran is a double-stranded small interfering ribonucleic acid (siRNA) that prevents proprotein convertase subtilisin/kexin type 9 (PCSK9) translation in the liver. This increases LDL-C receptor recycling and expression on the hepatocyte cell, which increases LDL-C uptake and reduces LDL-C concentrations in the circulation.

Pharmacokinetics
  Route Specific Pharmacokinetics
    Name
    Subcutaneous Route
    Description
    A mean Cmax of 509 nanograms/mL was attained in approximately 4 hours post dose at the recommended dosing regimen. Concentrations were undetectable after 24 to 48 hours post dose. Mean AUC from dosing extrapolated to infinity was 7,980 nanograms/mL x hour. The pharmacokinetics of inclisiran were similar after single- and multiple-dosing.   Text
  Inclisiran is administered subcutaneously. It is 87% protein bound in vitro. Apparent Vd is approximately 500 L. Inclisiran has a high uptake into and selectively for the liver, the target organ for cholesterol lowering. Inclisiran is primarily metabolized by nucleases to shorter nucleotides of varying length. The terminal elimination half-life of inclisiran is approximately 9 hours, with no accumulation after multiple dosing. Approximately 16% of inclisiran is cleared renally.
 
After a single dose, LDL-C reduction was apparent within 14 days. Mean LDL-C reductions of 38% to 51% were observed 30 to 180 days post dose. At day 180, LDL-C concentrations were still reduced approximately 53%.

  Route Specific Administrations
    Administration Details
      Name
      Subcutaneous Administration
      Description
      Inclisiran should be administered by a health care professional.
Use caution during injection to avoid accidental needlestick injuries. The syringe needles do not have safety guards, and the needles are not removable.
Administration:
Clean the injection site with an alcohol wipe and allow the site to dry before injecting a dose.
Select an injection site in the abdomen, upper arm, or thigh. Do not inject in areas of active skin disease or injury (e.g., sunburns, skin rashes, or skin infection).
Air bubbles may be present in the prefilled syringe; this is normal.
Do not use the prefilled syringe if any of the seals on the outer carton or the plastic tray are broken; if the prefilled syringe looks damaged; if any of the injection solution has leaked out of the prefilled syringe; or if the prefilled syringe has been dropped after removing the needle cap. Do not try to take apart the prefilled syringe.
Do not remove the needle cap until you are ready to inject inclisiran. Early removal of the needle cap prior to injection can lead to drying out of the medication within the needle, which can result in needle clogging.
When ready for injection, firmly pull straight to remove the needle cap from the prefilled syringe. A drop of liquid may be present at the end of the needle; this is normal.
Once the needle cap is removed, do not put the needle cap back on.
Gently pinch the skin at the injection site and hold the pinch throughout the injection. With the other hand, insert the needle into the skin at an angle of approximately 45 degrees.
Continue to pinch the skin and slowly press the plunger as far as it will go; this will ensure that a full dose is injected.
Note: Use a new prefilled syringe if you are unable to depress the plunger after needle insertion.
Once the full dose is administered, remove the prefilled syringe from the injection site.
Dispose of the inclisiran syringe and needle cap into an appropriate sharps container.
The inclisiran prefilled syringe is a single use system; discard after use.
    Name
    Injectable Administration
    Description
    Visually inspect parenteral products for particulate matter and discoloration prior to administration whenever solution and container permit. Inclisiran is a clear and colorless to pale yellow solution. Do not use if particulate matter or discoloration is present.
Missed dose: If a planned dose is missed by less than 3 months, administer inclisiran and maintain the patient's original dosing schedule. If a planned dose is missed by more than 3 months, start with a new dosing schedule. Administer inclisiran initially, again at 3 months, then every 6 months.
