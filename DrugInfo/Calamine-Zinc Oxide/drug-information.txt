    General Information
    Xerosis (drying of the skin), skin irritation, or skin sensitivity may occur with calamine; zinc oxide lotion. Following topical application of calamine; zinc lotion, the powder component may clump and become abrasive due to evaporation of the water component. The lotion may become gritty in moist, intertriginous areas of skin, which some individuals may not tolerate. Instruct individuals to cleanse the affected area to remove any powder clumps prior to re-application.

Description
  Text
  Calamine; zinc oxide is a topical skin protectant and mild astringent. Calamine contains zinc oxide or zinc carbonate with ferric oxide. The lotion is used for the temporary relief of pruritis due to minor skin irritation and insect bites or stings, and to relieve itching and dry the oozing and weeping associated with Rhus dermatitis due to poison ivy, oak, or sumac. Calamine; zinc oxide cream or ointment is used for the treatment or prevention of diaper dermatitis. Systemic absorption is minimal following topical application of calamine; zinc oxide.

Mechanism Of Action
  Text
  Calamine: Calamine lotion contains zinc oxide or zinc carbonate with ferric oxide and is frequently used for symptomatic relief in pruritus because of its soothing properties. Its mild astringent and cooling effects relieve pruritus and dry out weeping skin lesions. It also acts as a skin protectant.
Zinc Oxide: Zinc oxide acts as a topical skin protectant and mild astringent. It inhibits mast cell degranulation and thereby reduces the secretion of histamine, an important mediator of inflammatory response and an inducer of pruritis.

      Lotion
Shake the lotion well before each use.
Cleanse the affected area with soap and water and allow the skin to dry before application.
Apply using a clean cotton ball or soft cloth; avoid vigorous rubbing over excoriated or broken skin.
Do not apply to deep or puncture wounds, animal bites, or serious burns; ask a doctor before use in these situations.
Discard if the product separates and does not re-mix on shaking, or if color or consistency changes.
 
Cream or Ointment
Change wet or solid diaper promptly.
Cleanse the area and allow to dry.
Apply cream or ointment liberally as often as necessary, with each diaper change, especially at bedtime or anytime when exposure to wet diapers may be prolonged.
