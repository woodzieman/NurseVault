    Treatment-Related Restrictions of Use
    General Information
    Prior to treatment initiation with icotrokinra, complete all age-appropriate vaccinations according to current immunization guidelines. Avoid the use of live vaccines in individuals receiving icotrokinra. No data are available on the response to live or inactive vaccines.
    Headline
    infection
    General Information
    Avoid icotrokinra treatment in individuals with any clinically important active infection until the infection resolves or is adequately treated. Consider the benefits and risks of icotrokinra treatment in individuals with a chronic infection or a history of recurrent infection. Advise individuals to report signs or symptoms of a clinically important infection. If an individual develops an infection and/or is not responding to standard therapy, discontinue icotrokinra and monitor the individual until the infection resolves. Evaluate individuals for tuberculosis infection (TB) prior to initiating icotrokinra based on clinical judgment. Avoid the use of icotrokinra in individuals with active TB. Consider anti-TB therapy prior to initiating icotrokinra in individuals with a past history of latent or active TB in whom an adequate course of treatment cannot be confirmed. Monitor individuals for signs and symptoms of active TB during and after icotrokinra treatment.
    Headline
    pregnancy
    General Information
    There are no data on the use of icotrokinra during pregnancy to evaluate for a drug-associated risk of major birth defects, miscarriage, or adverse outcomes. In animal studies, oral administration of icotrokinra in rabbits at a dose of 157 times the maximum recommended human dose (MRHD) based on AUC comparison resulted in maternal body weight loss, low food consumption, late pregnancy loss, and an increased fetal incidence of fused ribs. There is a pregnancy safety study to monitor outcomes. If an individual becomes pregnant while receiving icotrokinra, health care providers can report icotrokinra exposure by calling 1-800-526-7736 or visiting www.ICOTYDE.com.
    Headline
    breast-feeding
    General Information
    Use icotrokinra with caution during breast-feeding. There are no data on the presence of icotrokinra in human milk, the effects on the breastfed child, or the effects on milk production. During animal studies, icotrokinra was detected in the plasma of nursing rat pups. No adverse developmental effects were observed. When a drug is present in animal milk, it is likely that the drug will be present in human milk. Consider the benefits of breast-feeding, the clinical need for treatment, and any potential adverse effects on the breastfed child from the medication or from the underlying maternal medical condition.

    Headline
    candidiasis, infection
    General Information
    Medicines that interact with the immune system, such as icotrokinra, may increase the risk of infection. Fungal infections occurred in 1.1% of individuals (n = 1,296) receiving icotrokinra during clinical trials. The fungal infections included tinea pedis, tinea versicolor, oral candidiasis, onychomycosis, skin candida, urinary tract candidiasis, vulvovaginal candidiasis, genital infection, fungal ear infection, and fungal laryngitis. Two individuals experienced more than 1 event. If a clinically important active infection develops, discontinue icotrokinra until the infection resolves or is adequately treated.

    Headline
    antibody formation
    General Information
    Anti-drug antibody formation to icotrokinra occurred in 9.6% of adult and pediatric subjects during clinical trials. No neutralizing antibodies were detected. Among individuals who developed anti-drug antibodies, population pharmacokinetic analysis using pooled data showed that icotrokinra Cmax and AUC increased by 1.2-fold and 1.3-fold, respectively. There was no identified clinically relevant effect of anti-drug antibodies on pharmacokinetics, pharmacodynamics, safety, or effectiveness of icotrokinra over the treatment duration of 52 weeks.

Description
  Text
  Icotrokinra is a peptide that is an interleukin-23 (IL-23) receptor antagonist, and represents the first medication in its class. It is indicated for the treatment of moderate-to-severe plaque psoriasis in individuals who are candidates for systemic therapy or phototherapy. It selectively binds to the IL-23 receptor and inhibits IL-23 mediated inflammatory signaling, reducing the release of pro-inflammatory cytokines involved in psoriasis pathogenesis. During clinical trials, icotrokinra demonstrated significantly greater efficacy than placebo, with markedly higher proportions of individuals achieving Investigator's Global Assessment (IGA) 0/1 and Psoriasis Area and Severity Index (PASI) 90 responses by Week 16. Clinical responses were sustained through Week 24 and up to Week 52 in individuals who continued therapy, with consistent efficacy observed across adult and pediatric populations and in those with psoriasis of the scalp and genital regions. Icotrokinra may increase the risk for infection. Consider evaluating individuals for tuberculosis infection prior to initiating treatment. The most common adverse reactions include headache, nausea, fungal infection, and fatigue. Treatment for plaque psoriasis is individualized, and considers treat to target strategies and severity of disease, including the presence or absence of psoriatic arthritis. Icotrokinra was FDA-approved in March 2026.

Mechanism Of Action
  Text
  Icotrokinra selectively binds to the interleukin-23 receptor (IL-23R) and antagonizes the binding of IL-23 to the receptor. IL-23 is a naturally occurring cytokine that is involved in inflammatory and immune responses. Icotrokinra inhibits the IL-23/IL-23R-dependent release of pro-inflammatory cytokines.
 
Pharmacodynamics
In exploratory analyses of pharmacodynamic markers from clinical studies, icotrokinra lowered serum levels of pro-inflammatory cytokines, including IL-17A, IL-17F, IL-19, IL-22, and beta-defensin-2, which are linked to psoriasis. It also reduced expression of genes in the IL-23/Th17 pathway in lesional skin biopsies, including IL17A, IL17F, IL19, IL22, IL23A, and DEFB4A. The relationship between these pharmacodynamic markers and the mechanism by which icotrokinra exerts its clinical effects is not fully understood.

Pharmacokinetics
  Route Specific Pharmacokinetics
    Name
    Oral Route
    Description
    Icotrokinra Cmax and AUC increase in a dose-proportional manner between 0.05 to 5 times the recommended dosage in healthy individuals. Following oral administration of icotrokinra 200 mg, the mean (standard deviation) Cmax and AUC are 3.62 (1.48) nanograms/mL and 44.8 (11.4) nanograms x hour/mL, respectively. Following multiple dose administration, accumulation for Cmax was up to 1.6-fold and for AUC was up to 1.5-fold. Icotrokinra steady-state is reached in approximately 3 days. The median time to maximum plasma concentration (Tmax) is 2 (0.25, 8) hours. Icotrokinra Cmax and AUC decreased by 59% and 43%, respectively, when administered with a high-fat meal (1,000 calories, 50% fat). Administration of caffeine did not affect the pharmacokinetics of icotrokinra.

  Text
  Icotrokinra is administered orally. It is 52% bound to plasma protein. The blood to plasma partition ratio is 0.53. The apparent (oral) Vd is 92,800 L. Icotrokinra is a peptide and is metabolized by peptide catabolism into smaller peptides. Icotrokinra has an apparent oral clearance of 6,550 L/hour. Following oral administration to healthy subjects, approximately 37% to 81% of the dose was recovered in feces within 24 hours as unchanged icotrokinra, and 0.001% of the dose was recovered in urine as unchanged icotrokinra. The median elimination half-life of icotrokinra is 12 hours.
 
Affected cytochrome P450 isoenzymes and drug transporters: None
Icotrokinra does not inhibit CYP1A2, CYP2B6, CYP2C8, CYP2C9, CYP2C19, CYP2D6, CYP2E1, CYP3A4, P-gp, BCRP, OATP1B1, OATP1B3, OAT1, OAT3, OCT2, MATE1, MATE2-K, and BSEP. Icotrokinra does not induce CYP1A2, CYP2B6, and CYP3A4. Icotrokinra is not a substrate of P-gp, BCRP, OATP1B1, OATP1B3, OAT1, OAT3, OCT2, MATE1, and MATE2-K.

Administration
  General Information
  For storage information, see the specific product information within the How Supplied section.
  Route Specific Administrations
    Administration Details
      Name
      Oral Solid Formulations
      Description
      Administer icotrokinra upon waking on an empty stomach with water. Wait at least 30 minutes after taking icotrokinra before eating.
Swallow tablets whole. Do not crush, split, or chew tablets.
If an individual is unable to swallow tablets whole:
Place 1 tablet in a cup containing at least 120 mL (4 ounces) of water. It may take a few minutes for the tablet to disperse. The tablet may not disperse completely. The mixture may look yellow, milky, or cloudy, and small pieces may be seen in the water, which are safe to swallow.
Gently swirl the cup before drinking the full mixture.
Add at least 120 mL (4 ounces) of additional water to the cup and completely swallow the contents to make sure the whole dose is taken.
Complete administration of icotrokinra within 15 minutes of dispersion in water.
Missed dose: Take the missed dose as soon as possible and then return to the normal dosing schedule the following day.
