Contraindications And Precautions
  Headline
  Contraindications/Warnings/Precautions
  General Information
  Nedocromil is not a bronchodilator and is contraindicated in the treatment of acute bronchospasm or status asthmaticus.Nedocromil is classified pregnancy category B. Adequate and well controlled studies in pregnant women have not been done. Animal data suggests that nedocromil crosses the placenta but does not cause fetal harm. Nedocromil is distributed into breast milk when administered intravenously, but excretion into milk following administration by other routes is not certain. Use cautiously in breast-feeding women.Nedocromil sodium for ophthalmic use contains benzalkonium chloride as a preservative, and wearers of soft contact lenses should be advised against using the lenses during therapy. Benzalkonium chloride can be absorbed by soft contact lenses.The safety and efficacy of nedocromil sodium for ophthalmic use have not been established in children less than 3 years of age.

Adverse Reactions
  Headline
  Adverse Reactions
  General Information
  In general, nedocromil is well tolerated. Adverse effects usually are limited to the site of application.Oral inhalation useNedocromil has been well tolerated, with minimal adverse effects being reported. The most frequent side effects are nausea/vomiting (4%), bitter taste (12.5%), sore throat (5.7%), headache (6%), cough (7%) and rhinitis (4.6%). In comparisons with other antiasthmatic drugs, nedocromil produced fewer GI and CNS effects than theophylline and had similar side effects to cromolyn. Other statistically significant gastrointestinal adverse events occuring more frequently with nedocromil than with placebo are dyspepsia and abdominal pain. In a study of nedocromil vs placebo in 110 patients with asthma, only dysgeusia (29%) occurred significantly more often in patients receiving nedocromil by oral inhalation compared with patients receiving placebo (2%). Cough, dyspnea, and pharyngitis did not occur more often with nedocromil than after placebo.Ophthalmic useSide effects with ophthalmic administration are similar to those reported with the inhalation route. Headache (40%) is the most commonly reported side effect. Ocular irritation, burning, or stinging is reported in 10—30% of patients; dysgeusia, nasal congestion have also been reported at this incidence. Side effects reported in 1—10 % of patients include asthma, redness of the eyes, photophobia, and rhinitis.

Description
  Text
  Nedocromil is a mast cell stabilizer. It is available as an ophthalmic solution for the treatment of allergic conjunctivitis in adult and pediatric patients. Nedocromil ophthalmic solution is approved in patients 3 years and older.

Mechanism Of Action
  Text
  Nedocromil sodium is a mast cell stabilizer. Nedocromil sodium inhibits the release of mediators from cells involved in hypersensitivity reactions. Decreased chemotaxis and decreased activation of eosinophils have also been demonstrated. In vitro studies with adult human bronchoalveolar cells showed that nedocromil sodium inhibits histamine release from a population of mast cells having been defined as belonging to the mucosal subtype and inhibits beta-glucuronidase release from macrophages. In the eye, the drug reduces ocular itching due to allergies.

  Route Specific Administrations
    Name
    Ophthalmic Administration
    Description
    For ophthalmic use only.
Wash hands before and after use.
Tilt the patient's head back slightly and pull the lower eyelid down with the index finger. Squeeze the prescribed number of drops into the conjunctival sac. Have the patient blink a few times to spread the solution. Have patients close eye and remove any excess solution with a clean tissue. Repeat in the other eye.
Avoid contamination. Do not touch the tip of the dropper to the eye, fingertips, or other surfaces.
To avoid the spread of infection, do not use a dropper for more than one person.
