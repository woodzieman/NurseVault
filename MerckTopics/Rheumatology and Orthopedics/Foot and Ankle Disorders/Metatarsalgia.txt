=== Metatarsalgia (Merck Manual Professional) ===

Metatarsalgia is a general term for pain in the area of the metatarsophalangeal joints. (See also Overview of Foot and Ankle Disorders.)

Most common causes include:

Freiberg disease

Metatarsal stress fracture

Interdigital (Morton) neuroma

Metatarsophalangeal joint pain

Sesamoiditis

Submetatarsal head fat pad atrophy typically associated with aging
